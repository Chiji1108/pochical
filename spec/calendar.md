# Calendar

How カレンダー, the person's own month, behaves on screen: what each part shows, when, and what a tap does. The logic it runs on is spec/shift-patterns.md's, pinned by spec/vectors; this is what a screen adds around it, so every platform's screen does the same. /design's calendar (`packages/prototype/src/components/design-calendar.tsx` and its parts) shows it.

## The month

- A page a month (a period, with a シフトの期間: The shift period, below), turned by swiping. 今月 is offered in the heading while another month is shown, and brings this month back. The heading's buttons (今月, the save menu, 閉じる) are glass with their words or symbol in the label color; only 完了, which ends entering, is filled with the accent (spec/chat.md, The chat's page). The month's name, while neither entering nor in a day's week, opens 月を選ぶ, as months are picked elsewhere; it draws no chevron, as large as it is.
- 設定 > カレンダー's 月名をタップしたとき can make the name's tap おたのしみ instead (kept with the account): a sky of pale light at the top of the month, over its heading and gone by its first weeks, three lights warm to cool across it (`design/src/skies.ts`, written out for the apps by `mise run gen`). It first comes with a tap, never before, not even in the settings' preview; each tap drifts it to another, kept until the next, so a sky someone likes can be screenshotted. The first is the テーマ's own; the rest come at random among those anyone may get and the テーマ's own. Only a tap changes it: a テーマ's sky stays after the テーマ changes. Pale in light mode, deep a step lighter than the screen in dark mode (月夜's and 黒板's own grounds), it settles in under a second and breathes slowly while it stays, still with reduced motion. On iOS 月を選ぶ is a popover of the system's year and month wheels under the name, turning the pages as they stop, so the month stays in sight; a tap outside closes it. Android has no picker of months alone, so there it is Material's date picker in a dialog, opened on the month shown: a day picked turns the pages to its month, and so does 表示 to the month the picker shows.
- The heading follows the pages as they move, by a finger or on their own: the month rolls within its line toward the one coming in, the next up from below and the one before down from above (an English month's name a letter at a time, each a little after the one before; numbers and Japanese months whole), and the year only when it changes; 今月 (今週 in a day's week) fades in as the pages leave this month and out as they come back to it. With reduced motion the names just change.
- Each page shows the whole weeks that cover its month, from the week's first day (spec/vectors/month.json), and keeps room for six weeks, the most a month spans, so what is under the grid stays put as months turn. A day is 64pt tall where the screen has room; on a short one (iPhone SE) the days shorten so the six weeks fit between the heading and what is under them, which keep their size (44pt at least). The order's calendar (繰り返し) fits the same way. The days of the months around it show faded, with what they hold.
- Today is framed inside its day, and its date drawn in the accent. While entering (ポチポチ入力), the frame gives way to the day being entered; the accent date stays.
- The day being entered, or the day opened, is framed in the accent, more strongly than today.
- A day with a memo has a highlighter stroke under its date, shift or not (spec/shift-patterns.md, A day's memo).
- Today moves on at midnight while the screen is open.
- Marks are drawn in the person's スタイル (設定, kept with the account, spec/sync-protocol.md, Preferences): icons filled or outlined, emoji, or letters on a tile; and for each shape its own options, kept as each was left: the shift's name under its mark, and 休みの見せ方 for each pattern that counts as off, so 休み can be left blank while 有休 stands out: 強調 (on a tint of its color), 印だけ (its mark alone) or 空白 (left blank), which comes back faint while entering and in a day's week, where the days are what is being looked at. スタイル shows one choice for every such pattern, each drawn with its own mark and named once there is more than one; a pattern not set yet shows as the shape starts (強調 for icons, 印だけ for emoji and letters).
- The screens are drawn in the person's テーマ (設定 > スタイル, kept with the account; the テーマ three to a page, swiped, the pages as tall as their cards at the reader's text size, opening on the page of the one in use), in light or dark as 外観 says (kept on the device: the phone's by default, or always one of them; its page is iOS's 画面表示と明るさ: ライト and ダーク side by side, each a small screen in the テーマ with a circle under it, and 端末に合わせる under them, on by default, to follow the phone (not iOS's own 自動, which switches by the time of day; オンにすると、端末の設定に合わせてライトとダークが切り替わります。 under it); with it on the one the screen is in is checked, picking one turns it off, and turning it off keeps the one in use; on Android it is Android's own dark theme setting's, 端末に合わせる, ライト and ダーク as three choices with the same words under them, and the splash and the system's own sheets follow it from Android 12 on), but for the テーマ drawn dark whatever 外観 says, which turns the screens dark. シフトの色 ワントーン draws every shift in the テーマ's own color, the palette's first; emoji keep theirs.
- 設定 and the pages under it list their rows as each platform's own settings do: iOS's inset grouped lists; on Android, Material 3 Expressive's, as Android 16's Settings draws them: each section a rounded group of segmented rows, a small gap apart with the larger corners on the first and last, each row led by its symbol (Material Symbols Rounded; a face, mark or check where the row is someone or something), one line where it can be, its value and › at its end (↗ for a row that leaves the app), and the one picked of a choice on the selected container; under a flexible app bar whose large title goes into the bar as the list scrolls. A choice among a few words is a connected button group. /design draws them the same (SettingsLists), and on every Android screen its switches as Material 3's, its choices among a few words as the connected button group, and a page's title going into the bar as what is under it scrolls; choices that show samples, like スタイル's, stay as iOS's segments there, where the app draws selection cards.
- スタイル shows this week and the next in the look being set, and each テーマ on a card; カレンダー shows this week under the month's heading. Their days are a made-up run of the person's own patterns (each working one with what follows it, like 明け after 夜勤, and a day off after every second one and after each one followed, ending on a day off; `spec/vectors/style-preview.json`), the same whatever they have entered, so a change shows in the same places every time; no memo, 早出 or 残業 shows there. Swiped aside, the preview shows the home screen with the two weeks' widget drawn from the same days (/design's StylePreview: the カレンダー widget at its medium size, on iOS drawn by the widget's own view and on Android at a phone's 4×2 by Glance itself; a picture, which no touch opens), its page named at the preview's top end (カレンダー, ウィジェット) with its dots under it. ☀︎ / ☾ on the preview's edge shows it and the テーマ cards in the other of light and dark, without touching 外観; a テーマ drawn dark keeps its preview dark, its ☾ on and not to be turned. Every preview of the calendar has its own ☀︎ / ☾, and no other place does.
- The week starts on the day the person sets (設定 > カレンダー, kept with the account). Saturdays and Sundays color the weekdays' heading and holidays their date, in Sunday's red, each unless the person turns it off.
- Under the month, while neither entering nor in a day's week, 今月のお休み (〇月のお休み away from this month) counts the month's days whose pattern counts as off, its month and number crossfading in place as the pages move (the heading over them rolls, so the screen does not move everywhere at once), and opens 今月の内訳: each of the person's patterns, in their order, with its days in the month, then 未入力 for the days with no shift (a day with only a memo among them), and the month's length under them (この月は全〇日); what they count is pinned by `spec/vectors/month.json` (summary). /design also lists 一緒に働く人 there, each with their days in the month, picking one to fade the days they are not on; the apps leave that for later.
- Holidays are those of the device's holiday country: its region's when Pochical has that country's holidays (`design/scripts/holidays.ts`), else Japan's, its home. Every date colored as a holiday reads this one country, and a new order's 祝日は休みにする takes it too (spec/shift-patterns.md, Holidays).

## The shift period

Shifts are often handed out for a period that is not a month, 15日 to the next 14日, as a workplace closes its month on its own day. 設定 > カレンダー's シフトの期間 (kept with the account, spec/sync-protocol.md, Preferences; asked of a roster's work by はじめの設定 and 新しい仕事にする, spec/shift-patterns.md, Changing jobs) is 1日〜末日, the month, until the person picks a day from 2日 to 28日 for it to start on (15日〜翌月14日; no later, so every month has the day), a period running from that day to the day before it a month on (`spec/vectors/periods.json`). Most people's shifts come by the month, and for them nothing changes.

With a period set, each page of the calendar is a period rather than a month, so what is shown, counted, entered and handed out is always the same days: the page, 今月のお休み and its 内訳, where ポチポチ入力 ends and what 完了 asks about, and what the picture and the device calendar get. What this spec says of the calendar's month is said of the period, but for what follows. It is one calendar, the range of its page an input: never a second calendar to pick, which every screen would then need twice.

- The page draws the whole weeks holding the period from the week's start, as a month's does (a period of 31 days spans six weeks at most, as a month does), the days outside it faded with what they hold, as those of the months around are. The 1st of the month inside it is dated 11/1, with 月と曜日 in English too (a number, not a month's name, and short as a day's room is), so the page shows where the month turns.
- The heading names the period by its days, 10/15〜11/14 (oct. 15 – nov. 14 in English, as the heading writes months), rolling as a month's name does, with the year of its first day, and of its last when that is another. 今月 is 今日, as the page it brings back is the one holding today, which is the page the calendar opens on. 月を選ぶ's month opens the period starting in it.
- Under it, この期間のお休み counts the page's days off, its number crossfading as the month's does; it opens 期間の内訳, its title's subtitle the period with its year (2026/10/15〜11/14), and この期間は全〇日 under the counts.
- A screen reader hears 「期間の最後の日です。入力が終わったら完了を押してください」 on the period's last day, rather than 月末です.
- Saving names the period wherever it would name the month (Saving a month as a picture, Adding a month to the device calendar).
- Shared and shown with others, the rest keeps to months, as calendars do: the widgets (spec/widgets.md), a group's 月で見る and みんな休み, and the order's calendar (繰り返し), whose rows are the months of the order rather than anyone's shifts.

## ポチポチ入力

- ポチポチ入力 starts entering on the month's first blank day, the 1st when none is blank. A month swiped to while entering starts on its first blank day the same way; a day tapped is entered next, turning to its month when it is one of the months around.
- The tray holds the day being entered, in words with its weekday colored as the calendar's (not a button: another day is picked on the calendar above, a month away by swiping), a button for each pattern in the person's order (pages of `PATTERNS_PER_PAGE` past that many, `design/src/limits.ts`, swiped, as tall as the fullest page so what is under them stays put; the keys keep their size at every text size, as a keyboard's do, and at the accessibility sizes show large while held, where the platform has a way to: iOS's large content viewer; Android has none, so its keys stay as they are), 消す and 翌日へ. 消す is offered only when the day has a shift, and 翌日へ only before the month's last day.
- A pattern entered moves the selection on as spec/shift-patterns.md (The next day) has it.
- A screen reader hears what each key did and where entering goes on, as /design's live region says it: 「10月3日、日勤を入力しました。翌日は明けです。5日を選択中」, シフトを消しました for 消す, 変更せずに進みました for 翌日へ, and on the month's last day 「月末です。入力が終わったら完了を押してください」. A day is read with its hours when they differ from the pattern's (早出 7:00 – 16:00).
- 消す clears the day's shift, with its own hours and people; its memo stays (spec/shift-patterns.md, A day's memo). The tray is a keyboard, so it asks nothing.
- 完了 ends entering, then asks about the month's blank days before its last entered one (spec/shift-patterns.md, Blanks when entering ends). It asks rather than explains, as a blank day is most often a day off not entered: この{n}日はお休みですか？ (この日はお休みですか？ for one), the days (1日(日), and a month's 1st past the first month's, on a period's page, 11/1(日) as the page dates it), and one button that answers by filling them, 〈the day off〉にする. While it asks, the calendar behind draws those days faint as the pattern picked would fill them, whatever the look, so the question points at them, and reads them as still 未入力; the sheet's own close leaves them blank, with nothing else to say the same. With more than one pattern that counts as off, chips pick which; the first is picked to begin with. Unless the pattern picked was shown blank as the sheet opened, カレンダーでは空白で見せる (お休みとして入れて、印は出しません) is offered beside it, so a person who would rather keep the days blank for the look fills them all the same; it turns that pattern's 休みの見せ方 to 空白 as the days are filled, and closing the sheet changes nothing. A day off left blank looks like a day not entered, and the way back is only in 設定, so the switch also says where, in the same lighter words and read with it as one: あとから設定の「スタイル」にある「休みの見せ方」で変えられます, in words as the support page says it rather than with ›, so a screen reader reads the way there as it is written. With no pattern that counts as off, nothing is asked.

## A day's detail

- Tapping a day, while not entering, opens it: the month folds to its week, the day framed, and the day's detail shows under it (at once, with reduced motion). On iOS the detail is the system's sheet: it rises to just under the folded week, which stays in sight to tap another day, grows to the whole screen when pulled up or while the memo is written, and swiped down or closed with its × it goes, the month unfolding behind it. Its bar holds the date, × and ‹ ›, and シフト and 一緒に働く人 push their lists within it, as a sheet's lists do on iOS; the heading's × is left to the sheet's. On Android the detail lies under the folded week on the screen itself, the tab bar giving way to it, with the date, ‹ › and × in its own row; Android's back, predictive or not, unfolds the month as a pull does, following the finger, and シフト's list is a page pushed over the screen. The month folds as one sheet, as the Calendar apps' does: it moves up to bring the week to the top while its other weeks fade, and unfolds back the same way, the detail going at once. The month shown stays while the week is one of its rows, a faded day's too.
- Another day of that week can be tapped, and ‹ and › beside the day's date open the day before and after, past a week's end too, so days can be gone through one after another; into another week, by them or 今週, the weeks slide to it as a swipe does, and the detail gives way to the day's however it is opened. Swiping moves a week, the weeks before and after sliding in with the finger as the months do, and opens the same weekday a week on or back once it settles; 今週 is offered while another week is open. Pulling the week down unfolds the month again, as × in the heading does (the sheet's on iOS), the month following the finger: let go past a third of the way, or flicked down, it unfolds, else it folds back. A pull, slow or flicked, never opens the day it starts on, and a tap that does not move always does.
- シフト is picked from the person's patterns in a list of its own, as the platforms pick one of many (a menu runs long past a few, and there may be up to `syncLimits.patterns`): in their order, each with its mark, name and hours, the day's checked; picking one goes back to the day. Past a page of ポチポチ入力's keys (`PATTERNS_PER_PAGE`), a search narrows them by name. Picking another keeps the day's memo and people, as entering does. A day with no shift shows なし.
- 時間 shows for a pattern with hours: the day's own start and end, each kept only where it differs from the pattern's, with the change said in words (早出, 残業, else 変更済み), and 標準に戻す while it differs.
- 一緒に働く人 shows for a pattern that does not count as off, naming those on the day (なし for none). How they are picked follows each platform's own way of choosing several: on Android and /design it unfolds into the person's coworkers as filter chips, in their order, picked or not, and 追加, staying unfolded as another day is opened, for noting people day after day; on iOS the row opens the coworkers as a list to check them in, as the Clock app's 繰り返し picks days, with 人を追加… at its foot, gone back from when done. Either adds while under `COWORKERS_MAX`, else says 一緒に働く人は{n}人までです; someone added there goes to the end of the list and is on the day too.
- The memo can be written on any day, shift or not, up to `textLimits.dayNote`, and is kept as text fields are (below). While it holds words, a clear button in the field empties it at once and keeps that, as a one-line field's does: the memo alone goes, the shift stays.
- この日のシフトを消す, with a shift, clears it with its own hours and people; the memo stays. When a time change or people would go with it, it asks first and names them: 「一緒に働く人も消えます。」.

## Saving a month as a picture

The month's heading has a way to save it (/design's save menu). Saving is never offered by itself, not even as a month is filled: a filled month shows as filled, and the heading's corner is there whenever it is wanted. 画像で保存 shows the month as it will go out: its name, the weekdays and its days drawn as the calendar draws them on the calendar's own ground, without today's frame or memo strokes, the days of the months around it faded and holding nothing, and ポチカル with the store's icon at its foot, so whoever gets it can find the app by the same dog. Its look is its own, apart from the person's スタイル: light or dark (the screen's until picked, kept on the device; an always-dark テーマ stays dark), and シフト名 and 休みの見せ方, kept with the account. Names start on, as it goes to people who do not know the marks. 休みの見せ方 is set for each pattern that counts as off, as on スタイル and as 端末カレンダーに追加 picks days off, those of a job left too, as 有休 may be worth showing family while 休み is not; each starts 強調, shown, as they could not tell an empty day from one not entered. 共有 opens the system's share sheet, 保存 puts it in Photos. 端末カレンダーに追加 is the menu's other way (below). A period's picture (The shift period) is titled by its days with its year, 2026年10月15日〜11月14日 (Oct 15 – Nov 14, 2026 in English; 2026年12月15日〜2027年1月14日 across a year), its days outside the period faded and holding nothing as a month's around it are, its month's 1st dated 11/1 as on the page; it is shared and read as 2026年10月15日〜11月14日のシフト.

## Adding a month to the device calendar

端末カレンダーに追加 puts the month's shifts (a period's, The shift period, named by its days where the month is named below: 10/15〜11/14のシフトを、1日ずつ予定として入れます。, ポチカルで入れた10/15〜11/14の予定を消す) in a calendar of the device, an event a day (`spec/vectors/device-calendar.json`): the shift's name, at the day's own hours else its pattern's, ending the next day when the end comes at or before the start, and all day for a pattern with no time. Only the patterns picked under 入れるシフト go in: a row of the sheet saying under its name what is left out, every name of it as a switch's line under it says what it does, rather than one cut off at the row's end (明け・休み・有休は入れません; すべて入れます, or 何も入れません), opening a page of every pattern in use (and one left that still shows in the month) by its mark, name and time, a check on those that go in, as a day's 勤務 is picked. Until the person picks otherwise, the patterns with a time go in, and of those without, all but the days off (a calendar shared with family need not hold every 休み) and a pattern another names as its next day, like 明け after 夜勤, whose night shows on that day already (`shownAtFirst` in the vectors); 非番, 待機, 出張 and the like go in. What the person changed from that is kept with the account, only for patterns still there. A day's memo and the names of those on it go in a shift's event's notes while メモも入れる and 一緒に働く人も入れる are on, and a day with a memo and no shift's event (no shift, or a pattern left out) gets an all-day event of its own named by the memo; each switch shows only when it would put something in (メモも入れる when a day of the month has a memo, 一緒に働く人も入れる when a day of a pattern put in has someone on it), the note that adding again replaces the month and the way to Google Calendar moving up under 入れるシフト when neither does, and each is off at first, as a calendar may be shared with family, and kept with the account as last left (the memo, then 一緒に働く人：A、B, as the vectors have it). The calendar is picked among those that take new events, grouped by account; the one added to last is picked first, else the system's default. The list follows the device's calendars while the sheet is open, so one turned on in 設定 meanwhile shows without opening the sheet again, and one that goes away while picked gives way to that first pick. Each event carries the app's link to its day and whose shift it is (`pochical://day/YYYY-MM-DD?by=<user id>`, spec/widgets.md), Pochical's mark on it. Adding a month again first takes out the events of its days that carry the user's own mark, in any calendar that takes changes, so a calendar shared with family keeps theirs, so the month is put in anew rather than twice, whichever device put them in, after a reinstall or a sync that changed their ids too; what changes later is not followed, and is put in by adding again. While some of those events of the month are in a calendar, a red ポチカルで入れた〇月の予定を消す under the add button, Pochical named so it is not read as every event of the month, takes them all out, as from a calendar picked by mistake, rather than one by one: asked once first (ポチカルで入れた予定を12件消します / 9月の分を、どのカレンダーからも消します。ほかの予定は消しません。, 消す), as they do not come back, then 消しました and how many. It needs the calendar's full access, to list the calendars and find its own events; refused, the sheet says where to allow it. Under the calendar, Googleカレンダーに入れるには opens the site's guide (pochical.app/support/google-calendar, linked from サポート too) in the system's browser sheet: a calendar of a Google account added to the iPhone carries the events on to Google Calendar, which few find by themselves, and the guide walks through adding the account in 設定, picking its calendar as 追加先, and what to check when they do not show. On Android the save corner is Material's menu of the two, and 端末カレンダーに追加 is a page of its own, 入れるシフト pushed over it with checkboxes and 追加先 picked in a dialog of radio rows under each account; it asks for the calendar's access (`READ_CALENDAR` and `WRITE_CALENDAR`, Android's whole of it) as the page opens, and refused, says where to allow it with 許可する while Android would ask again, else 設定を開く for the app's page in the system's settings. Its calendars are those of the phone's accounts that take events (`CalendarContract`), followed as the provider changes; the one picked first is the one added to last, else an account's own (`IS_PRIMARY`), else the first. The mark goes in the columns Android keeps for an app's own link on an event (`CUSTOM_APP_PACKAGE`, `CUSTOM_APP_URI`), so a calendar app opens the day from the event, and the events are put in and taken out in one batch of the provider, whole or not at all. A Google account's calendars are the phone's own on Android, so there is no guide to link to.

## Reminders

設定 › 通知 › リマインド holds reminders of the person's own shifts, which the device sends by itself as local notifications, kept on the device like alarms: several at once, each on or off, one to start with (前日 21:00). A reminder goes off the evening before a day with a shift at its time (前日), days off too, or some minutes before a shift with a time starts (開始前), as the day's own hours else its pattern's have it. Like an alarm's days, it keeps the shifts it skips, so a pattern added later is reminded of until it is turned off. What it says is the shift as its title, its 早出 or 残業 said (明日は日勤, あと1時間で夜勤（残業）), and its time under it (16:30 – 翌9:30), as `spec/vectors/reminders.json` pins with when each goes off. The days are read as everything reads them, the day's own else its order's.

A phone keeps only so many notifications waiting (iOS 64), so the nearest ones, two weeks ahead, are put in the system's queue, and all are put in anew whenever the days, the patterns, the reminders or the permission change, and each time the app comes back. Android has no such queue: the system wakes the app at the next one (AlarmManager), the app shows what is due on the reminders' own channel (リマインド, beside the chats') and sets the one after, set anew whenever the days, the patterns, the reminders or the permissions change, sync's changes among them, and as the device starts, the app is updated, or the clock or its time zone changes, which take the system's alarms away or move them. A reminder says its minute (21:00に通知が届きます, 開始の15分前), so its alarm is exact, with the special access Android 14 leaves to the person (アラームとリマインダー, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`); without it the system may bring one up to an hour late. Taking that access away in the system's settings stops the app and cancels its exact alarms, and nothing tells the app until it next runs, so an inexact alarm for the same minute is set beside each exact one: the system keeps it, and the reminder comes up to an hour late rather than not at all; whichever comes first shows it, and the other finds it shown. One due that the system has not brought yet is kept as the next is set, so it comes late rather than never, up to that hour; one later than that, as after the device was off past it, is let go, as a notification is of no use once its evening or its shift has moved on. Nothing arrives until the system allows it; the page leads with asking, or once refused with the way to the system's settings. On Android, once notifications are allowed and while a reminder is on, the page then asks for exact alarms while the system has not given them (時刻ちょうどに届けるには, with 許可する opening the system's アラームとリマインダー for the app), and goes on without them, the reminders coming late. Turning a reminder on or saving one puts the system's question where it can still be put (Android 13's, until turned down for good), as the offer's お知らせする does.

The first time ポチポチ入力 is closed with shifts entered (after the blank days' sheet, when it asks), or as the calendar next shows after はじめの設定 or 新しい仕事にする set up a repeating order (which fills the days without ポチポチ入力), while the system has not been asked (on Android, while Android 13's question has never been put; before Android 13, where notifications need no asking, it is not offered) and a 前日 reminder is on, the calendar offers the reminders once, in few words: 前の晩に、明日のシフトをお知らせしますか？ and that reminder's time (21:00に通知が届きます。), with あとで and お知らせする. お知らせする brings the system's question; あとで lets it be. It is never offered again, whatever the answer, and kept on the device only. The store's review is not asked that time (spec/review.md).

## How days are written

Each platform writes a day, its weekday and its month through one set of helpers, so another language changes them in one place: Swift's `DayText.swift` in PochicalKit, and on the web `design-days.ts` (`formatDay` and its kin) with `design-week.tsx`'s weekday names. In Japanese they are 11月1日(日) in full, 11月1日, 1日(日) where the month goes without saying, 11/1 where room is short, a year before them (2026年…, 2026/…) for a day of another year, and 2026年11月 or 11月 for a month; a period (The shift period) as 10/15〜11/14, 2026/10/15〜11/14 where its year shows, its last day's year too when that is another (2026/12/15〜2027/1/14), and 2026年10月15日〜11月14日 in full, never by a month's name, which would read as the month's own days. Screens never put a day's numbers together themselves.

設定 > カレンダー's 月と曜日 (日本語 or 英語, kept with the account) names the months and weekdays of the headings and short labels in English, as lettered calendars do: the calendar's month as sep. (lower case, cut to three letters with a period, may whole), over its year, with no 月; a month with its year as September 2026, alone as September, and in small text as Sep; a weekday over a column of days as one letter (S M T W T F S), where the column's place tells T from T, beside a date as Thu (24 Thu, 9/24 Thu), and as three capitals (THU) over columns that do not start the week, like the hub's run from today. The month wheels of 月を選ぶ name months as the headings do. Sentences stay Japanese, their dates with them (a day's detail's 9月24日(木), 〜から, 〇月のみんな休み), as do 週の始まり's names and the screen readers' labels. The widgets' words follow it too (spec/widgets.md).

## Text fields

Free text (a day's memo, a name) is kept when the field is left, its screen closes or the app goes to the background, not on each change, so a memo typed is one edit in the outbox (spec/sync-protocol.md, Outbox) rather than one a letter.
