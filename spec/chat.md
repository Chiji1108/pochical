# Chat

How chat messages behave beyond sync (spec/sync-protocol.md): which words are links and mentions, how a link's preview is made, when a mention notifies, editing and unsending, and reporting and blocking. Both native apps and the server follow it. The web prototype's `textParts` in `packages/prototype/src/lib/chat-text.ts` (pinned by `spec/vectors/chat-text.json`) and the chat in `/design` follow this spec.

The numbers named `chatRules.*` here are in `design/src/chat.ts`, the one place they are written; `mise run gen` gives the apps the same values.

## Where a chat is kept

A chat is kept in a place: a group, whose chats are 全体チャット and its one-to-one chats, or a user's chat with Pochical's people. Every place keeps its chats alike, in a Group DO (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat), so a chat's lines, pages, outbox, read marks, notifications and photos work the same in each; what a place's chats can do differs only by its rules, `chatPlaces` in `design/src/chat.ts`, which the server and both apps read:

- `directChats`: any two members have a one-to-one chat beside the place's own; without it the place's only chat is its own (thread "group").
- `days`: lines share days (日にちを共有), `polls`: days are put to the vote, `pins`: lines are pinned, `edit`: words are changed after sending (編集), `mentions`: words mention a member, `reports`: lines and members are reported and blocked.
- `sendsDevice`: each line goes with the app's version and the device (`ChatSend.device`), kept for whoever reads the chat, as its head says.

The server drops an edit its place does not take (one in a one-to-one chat, a line of days or a poll, 編集, a pin, a vote or settling a poll) and refuses a report; the apps leave each out of their menus and tools. Within a group, what a one-to-one chat lacks (polls, mentions) is the thread's, not the place's.

## What a line is

A line is one thing to its reader, so it is one kind of thing in the data: the server, the wire (`ChatLine.content`, a `ChatContent`, and a send's `ChatSend.content`, a `ChatSendContent` of the first four kinds) and both apps hold a line as exactly one of these, a line waiting to be sent too, each with only what that kind has, and everything that treats lines differently goes by the kind alone.

- **words** (`ChatWords`): 1 to `textLimits.chatMessage` characters, not blank; 編集済み once changed; its first link's page, if any (Previews). Only words are changed (編集).
- **photos** (`ChatAlbum`): 1 to `chatRules.photosPerSend` photos sent at once, with no words (Photos, Albums).
- **days** (`ChatSharedDays`): 1 to `SHARED_DAYS_MAX` days shared with everyone's shifts, with no words (Long messages and shared days).
- **poll** (`ChatPoll`): `POLL_MIN_DAYS` days or more put to the vote, who can come on each, and the day settled on (Polls).
- **notice** (`ChatNotice`): a line from the app (Notices).
- **unsent**: taken back by its writer (送信取消). Nothing of what it was is left, whatever its kind was, as Telegram, LINE and iMessage show one line in its place; it answers no line and is pinned nowhere.
- **hidden**: how a one-to-one line from someone the reader blocked comes to them (Reporting and blocking): it shows nothing. A device that gets a line of a kind it does not know (from an app newer than itself) shows it as hidden, until the app is updated and takes its lines again (spec/sync-protocol.md, Change log and cursor).

What any line has besides is not its kind: who wrote it, when, its place, its reactions, the line it answers and its pin. A notice takes none of the last three, and a line taken back loses them.

## The chat's page

- A chat opens over the tabs, as LINE's and Telegram's do: pushed from the right with the screen under it, tab bar and all, so the tab bar goes away with that screen and comes back with it as the chat goes, a back swipe followed by the finger. Every page a tab opens comes the same way (the group's hub's みんなのシフト, settings, メンバーを招待 and グループを作る, and each page of 設定 and what it opens), as does what the chat opens (写真, みんなのシフト, the group's settings, another chat), and back goes back through them to the tab it was opened from, as that tab left it. The app has one navigation and one bar, which the tab's title and buttons are in too; the bar stays as pages come and go, so each page's top edge effect is there from the start of its push to the end of its pop (only the calendar, which its month heads, has none while it shows alone). On Android each page brings its own top app bar, as Material 3's pages do: there is no edge effect for one bar to keep, a page slides in from the end, and a predictive back is Android's own, the page shrinking as the finger goes to show the screen under it, tab bar and all. A notification's chat opens over its tab alone (グループ for a group's chat, 設定 for the chat with Pochical's people), in place of the pages open; a widget's or a link's day or group goes back to the tabs first.
- The title is the group's name for 全体チャット, with how many are in it under it, or the other member's name for a one-to-one chat. A tap on it opens what the chat is of: the group's settings, or the member's profile. While the group's socket is not open (接続中…) or its catch-up is still coming (更新中…, spec/sync-protocol.md, Keepalive) for longer than `socketRules.statusShownAfterMs`, that is said under the title instead, as LINE, Telegram and WhatsApp say it, so lines shown are not taken for all there are; it goes as soon as the socket is current.
- A member's profile, from their face, their mention or a one-to-one chat's title, is a sheet: their face, which opens large when it is a photo, their name, ブロック中 while blocked, 〇〇でのプロフィール with the group's mark, and メッセージを送る for the one-to-one chat with them, but in that chat itself and while they are blocked. ブロック and 通報 are in its ⋯ (Reporting and blocking).
- A one-to-one chat with someone who left the group, or was taken out of it, stays in the hub's chats to be read, but takes no more lines: 〇〇はグループを抜けました stands where the composer was, in a capsule of its own (glass on iOS) (相手のアカウントは削除されました once their account is deleted, spec/sync-protocol.md).
- ⋯ beside the title holds みんなのシフト for 全体チャット and 写真 for every chat (the chat with Pochical's people too, Photos), then the chat's notifications (Mentions).
- The bar's buttons (back and ⋯) and the round ↓ draw their symbols in the label color (the system's on iOS), on the system's glass there, as every bar button in the app does (the calendar's heading, the group's hub's 招待 and 設定); the accent is kept for a page's one confirming action, which fills with it as iOS 26 draws a prominent bar button (保存, 作る, 追加, 完了, 送る), and for the send button. The menus they open (⋯, the composer's ＋) draw their items in the label color too, a destructive one in the danger color.
- In the group's hub, under its chats, a group of one says メンバーを招待すると、1対1でも話せます。 Starting a one-to-one chat lists the members with 〇〇での名前とアイコンで話します。, as the chat belongs to the group.
- コピー says コピーしました; on Android 13 and later the system's own notice of what was copied says it, as Android asks of apps, and the app says nothing more.

## Opening a message's menu

A long press on a message (a right click on the web) opens its reactions and menu, as LINE, WhatsApp and iMessage do: the rest of the screen dims, the message stays where it was, lifted a little, with the reactions in a bar over it and the menu under it, or over the bar when there is no room under it, as LINE turns it; only when neither fits are they all moved together to stay on the screen (a message too tall for the room drawn smaller about its top corner by the writer, as the system's context menu draws a tall preview, down to half its size and past that cut short at its end), and the message's place in the chat left empty meanwhile. A keyboard up stays up as it opens, as LINE keeps it, when the message, its bar and its menu fit over it with the message where it was; typing there closes the menu. When they do not (a small screen, a tall message), the keyboard goes down for the room, as iMessage puts it down. Should the chat move under it meanwhile (the keyboard going down, lines coming in), the message, its bar and its menu go along with it. A tap outside closes it, and so does the app leaving the screen or the message going from the chat meanwhile (taken back by its writer), as the system's context menu goes with what it was opened on; a pick acts as it closes, as the system's context menu does, so what it opens (the keyboard for 返信) comes in while the menu goes, quick at first and settling, rather than after it. On the phones the finger that opened it may stay down and slide onto a reaction or a menu item, which lights up under it with a light tick; lifting there picks it, but only once the finger has slid (as far as the system's long press lets a finger wander), never when the menu came to a still finger, and lifting anywhere else leaves it open for a tap, as the system's context menu does. The apps draw it themselves rather than the system's context menu, which puts reactions inside the menu; on iOS the reactions' bar and the menu are the system's glass over the dimming, as Messages' are, the menu's items with no rule between them and its groups apart by a hairline, each item's symbol before its words (the words alone at the accessibility text sizes), both in the system's label color (送信取消 and 通報 in the danger color), its rows, groups and rule as tall as the system's menus have them, as those draw theirs. The press gives a little under the finger from the moment it goes down; held `chatRules.longPressMs`, the menu opens and a light haptic marks it, the two together. iMessage plays its haptic first and opens the menu a beat later, as a message moved in that beat is dragged instead; a line here is never dragged, so nothing waits between them. A tap is the message's own: a link or a mention opens, a photo opens large, a shared day or a poll's head does nothing. So scrolling past a line never opens its menu by accident. While the keyboard is up, a tap off the messages (beside or between them) puts it away, as LINE does, and so does dragging the lines down with it; a tap on a message stays the message's own. A long press on a link opens the link's menu instead. With a keyboard or a screen reader, activating the message opens its menu (a photo's activation opens it large; its menu is in the screen reader's actions, Screen readers).

## What is a link

- A link starts with `http://` or `https://` (any case) and runs over the characters a URL is written in (ASCII letters, digits and `-._~:/?#[]@!$&'()*+,;=%`). Any other character ends it, so Japanese written right after a link is not part of it: in `https://example.jp/ここどう？` the link is `https://example.jp/`.
- `.,!?:;'*` at its end belong to the sentence, not the link. So does a closing `)` or `]` with no opening one inside the link (`(https://example.jp)` → `https://example.jp`; `https://example.jp/A_(B)` keeps its bracket).
- A link needs a host; `https://` alone is not one.
- Addresses without a scheme (`example.jp`, `www.example.jp`), mail addresses and phone numbers are not links. Both apps use this rule instead of the platform's own detectors (`NSDataDetector`, `Linkify`), which find different things, so a message has the same links on every phone.

## In a message

- A message's bubble is round all over (`lg`), but the first of a run of one writer's (and a reply, a photo answering a line, and someone writing's dots): a tail at its top corner by the writer's side (top right for one's own, top left for others'), by their face and name, where LINE puts one, so it says whose words follow. It is one outline, never a tail laid on a corner: drawn for one's own W×H with r = `lg`, the top edge runs into the corner's arc, which goes down to a notch at x = W − 0.25r; from there a horn rises (a cubic, controls notch + (0.2r, −0.25r) and (W + 0.25r, 0.04r)) to its tip level with the top edge at (W + 0.6r, 0), and curves back in, concave (controls (W + 0.3r, 0.12r) and (W, 0.55r)), to the side at (W, min(1.5r, H − r)); the other corners are plain arcs, and others' bubbles are its mirror. The tail reaches past the bubble's frame: by the face every line of a run keeps 10 points for it, so the lines stay in line and it never touches the face; by the screen's edge it reaches into the margin, as LINE's and Messages' do, never cut off there. The same outline holds what the bubble holds (a link's page is cut to it), lights inside it when a line is jumped to (Pins), and is the bubble the long press lifts. Others' bubbles are on the left, one's own on the right. The face and the name stay at the top of the run. A run's lines sit close (`chatRules.lineGap`), and more room comes before a run's first line, a day's title and ここから新着 (`chatRules.runGap`), so each turn reads as one, as iMessage and LINE space them. The words are at body size, as Messages draws them. A line's time shows beside it, but for a line the next goes on the run of, sent in the same minute: that minute shows once, by the last of them, as LINE does. A line without its time keeps the time's room (and one being sent, its clock in that room), so a bubble is as wide either way and never widens as the next line comes. 編集済み and the pin stay on each line.
- Links are underlined; in others' messages in the accent color, in one's own in the bubble's text color.
- A tap on a link opens it in the system's browser sheet over the chat (`SFSafariViewController`, Custom Tabs).
- A long press on a link (or a right click) opens the link's own small menu, リンクを開く and リンクをコピー, as iOS offers on a link in text, without the reactions: on the web under the link, in the apps where the line's menu goes. It opens held as long as the line's menu (`chatRules.longPressMs`), and lifting the finger then opens nothing. The message's menu stays shorter without a link item.

## Large emoji

A message of nothing but 1 to `chatRules.largeEmojiMax` emoji shows them large and without a bubble, as iMessage does: drawn at `chatRules.largeEmojiSize` (points on iOS, sp on Android) at the reader's default text size, growing with it as body does. Each one is an emoji as a mark's is (spec/text-limits.md, `isEmoji`); a space, a line break or any other character among them keeps the message an ordinary bubble, and so does a reply, as the line it answers sits inside its bubble. `spec/vectors/chat-text.json` (largeEmoji) pins which messages count.

- Only how the message is drawn changes: it is sent and kept as words like any other, so an edit that leaves only emoji, or adds words to them, redraws it at once. The chat list's last line, a quote of it and its notification show it at the usual size.
- The time sits beside it, its reactions under it and its long press opens its reactions and menu, as on a bubble; a jump to it lights the emoji's own rounded ground, as a card's.

## Previews

A message carries at most one preview, for its first link. It is made while the message is written and sent with it, or by the server just after, so everyone sees the same preview and receivers never fetch the page.

- While writing, once the first link has stayed the same for `chatRules.linkPreviewSettleMs`, the app asks the server for its preview and shows it above the composer: the site's name, the title (読み込み中… until it arrives) and the picture small at the end, with × to send without it. × holds for that link until the message is sent or the link changes.
- A message sent before its preview arrives goes without one, and the server reads the page itself once it has the line, adding it to the line for everyone as if it had been sent with it; unless × took it off (`ChatSendWords.without_preview`), or the line is taken back or its first link changed meanwhile. If the server finds no page, nothing is shown above the composer, and the line stays without one.
- In the chat, the preview sits inside the message's bubble, under its words: the picture (cropped to `chatRules.linkPreviewAspect`, width over height), the title (two lines at most) and the site's name. A tap opens the link; a long press opens the message's reactions and menu.

## Pochical's invitation links

A link to `https://pochical.app/invite/{code}` (the host in any case, an optional trailing `/`, query and fragment ignored; `inviteCodeOf` in `packages/prototype/src/lib/chat-text.ts`) is one of Pochical's own invitations. The chat treats it as part of the app, not as a page:

- A tap on it, in the words or on its card, opens the group's join screen in the app, as reading its QR code does; when you are in the group already, it opens the group. Nothing opens in the browser, and nothing joins until you confirm on the join screen.
- Its card shows the group instead of a page: the group's mark at the hub's size on a tint, its name, and グループへの招待・{n}人 (参加中のグループ once you are in it). A long press opens the message's actions, as on a page's card.
- The card is not made while writing or sent with the message. Each app asks the server as the message shows (`GroupService.GetInvite`, which also says whether the reader is in the group), so a link that was remade or whose group was deleted turns into この招待は使えません for everyone, and a renamed group shows its new name. That card opens nothing; a tap on the link's words says the link cannot be used.
- Nothing is shown above the composer for it, and it takes the place of the message's one preview: a message whose first link is an invitation shows no page for later links.

## Reading a page

The server reads the page, so that people's addresses are not sent to the sites, and so both apps get the same result.

- The title is the page's `og:title`, else its `<title>`; the site's name is `og:site_name`, else the host without `www.`; the picture is `og:image`. A page with neither title gives its address without the scheme as the title.
- Only `http` and `https` on ports 80 and 443, to hosts that resolve to public addresses (no private, loopback or link-local ranges, checked again after each redirect). At most 3 redirects, 5 seconds, and the first 512 KB of HTML.
- The picture is fetched by the server (an image of at most 2 MB, as the site serves it) and stored with the preview, served to signed-in users from Pochical's own storage (`GET /v1/previews/{id}`), so it keeps showing after the site changes or removes it. A larger picture is left out.
- The server asks `ChatService.GetLinkPreview` with the link; the preview goes with the message (`ChatSendWords.preview`), and an edit says whether its first link stayed (`ChatChange.keeps_preview`) or gives the new link's page.
- Previews are cached by URL for a day.

## Mentions

A mention names one member of the group in a group chat (全体チャット). One-to-one chats have none.

- A message keeps a mention as `<@id>`, the member's id in the group, and shows it as @ and their name in the group as it is when read, so a later name change shows the new name. Copying a message, a chat's last line in the list, a quote and a notification show the same @name as plain words. A member who has left keeps their last name.
- While writing, an `@` (or the full-width `＠` a Japanese keyboard types) at the end of the message lists the other current members whose name contains what follows it (`spec/vectors/chat-text.json`, mentionQuery). Picking one writes `@name` and a space; only those picked become mentions when the message is sent, and only while `@name` is still followed by a space or the end. Typing a name by hand mentions no one.
- In a message, a mention is in the name's weight, in others' messages in the accent color; one of the reader looks the same, as the chat list's @ is what finds it. A tap on a mention opens that member's profile, except the reader's own.
- There is no mention of everyone: a group chat's line already reaches everyone, and a member who turned it off chose quiet.

### Notifications

- A group chat turned off sends no notifications, except for a line that mentions the reader, which notifies as if the chat were on. The chat notification settings say so under the group chats' switches, and so does a group's own 通知.
- Each chat's notifications turn off and on in its menu (通知をオフにする, 通知をオンにする, saying so as it changes); a group's chat also on 設定 › 通知 › チャット and in the group's own 通知. That page lists the one-to-one chats turned off as it opens, to turn back on, and those turned on again stay until it is left. A chat turned off shows a crossed-out bell after its name in the chat list and its title. The system's permission to notify is asked once, the first time a notification is wanted, on both platforms: as the person sends a line in a chat, turns a chat's notifications on, or takes the reminders; until it is allowed the device does not hear the server's notifications at all (its token is not sent, spec/sync-protocol.md, Push). The page shows a card while notifications are not allowed: to ask, or once refused, to open the system's settings.
- メンションはいつも通知 (設定 › 通知 › チャット › メンション, on by default) turns that exception off: off, a chat turned off sends nothing, mentions included, and the notes are not shown. It is one switch for the account, kept by the User DO, not one per group.
- In the chat list, a chat whose unread lines mention the reader shows @ in the accent color before the unread count.
- A line's notification is a message from its writer, as Messages, Signal and Telegram show one: the writer's face and name as the group shows them (their photo once the device has it, else their first letter, as everywhere), and for 全体チャット the group's name and mark as the conversation's, its words then worded as a one-to-one chat's (`CHAT_{…}`), as the system shows who wrote them already. A device that does not hold the group yet shows it as the server words it. A chat's notifications stack together, and a tap opens the chat at ここから新着, as from the chat list. An answer in the chat with Pochical's people is a plain notification instead, as only they write there: the app's icon, the title 作っている人とチャット (its row's name under 設定) and the answer's words, with no writer (The chat with Pochical's people); Android shows it as its conversations show any chat, each answer a message from 作っている人とチャット with the app's icon (spec/sync-protocol.md, Push).
- A line's notification is answered on it (返信), without opening the app, as in LINE and Messages: the words go as a new line of the reader's in that chat, not quoting the line, through the same outbox as the chat's composer, and the chat is read up to that line, the app icon's badge then following the counts as they come. Nothing is sent for empty words. The device waits until it is sent while the system gives it time; what has not gone by then goes when the app next opens. An answer the device cannot keep (as on a device out of storage) brings the line's notification back, saying 返信を送れませんでした and the words over the line's, to be answered again; the chat is not read then. An answer from Pochical's people is answered the same way. Android 15 keeps a notification answered on until it is let go of, so there it stays, showing the answer as the user's message (spec/sync-protocol.md, Push).
- Once lines are read, their notifications go from the device and from the user's other devices, as in Signal and Telegram: those of the chat's lines at or before where the reader read to (spec/vectors/chat.json, withdrawn). The device that read takes them back at once; the others are told by the server (spec/sync-protocol.md, Push), and, should that not reach them, take back what they know is read as their counts come: lines at or before the reader's own read mark they hold, or a chat whose count comes to nothing unread (a count the device held from before it opened may be older than the notifications since, so it is not one).
- A group the user leaves, or is taken out of, takes its chats' notifications from the device, and on Android their conversations (the shortcuts Android lists under Conversations), as none of its chats opens for them any more.
- A chat turned off is the same: its notifications, a mention's while メンションはいつも通知 is on, look, are answered and go the same way.

## Notices

全体チャット says, as a line from the app, what changes who is in the group, the way in, or what the group is, as LINE's groups do; nothing else. Someone joining (〇〇がグループに参加しました), leaving (〇〇がグループを抜けました, an account deleted while in the group too, named as deleted accounts are), being taken out by a member (〇〇が△△をグループから外しました) or by Pochical's people (〇〇がグループから外されました, the line's writer being who was taken out, spec/admin.md), the invite link remade (〇〇が招待リンクを作り直しました: a link someone already sent stops working, and this tells them why and to send the new one), and a new name or icon (〇〇がグループ名を「…」に変更しました, 〇〇がグループのアイコンを変更しました, or both: 〇〇がグループ名を「…」にして、アイコンを変更しました). A member's own name or photo, shifts and the table's layout are not said: they show where they are, and would flood the chat.

- The Group DO writes it as the change is made, a `ChatLine` of the notice kind (`ChatNotice`: what happened, who was taken out, the new name, whether the icon changed), its author being who did it, at the chat's next seq. Each device words it with the names the group shows now, those of members who left included.
- It sits centered in the tertiary color between the messages, as an unsent line does, and starts the run after it. The chat list says it as its last line.
- It is never unread and never notified (spec/sync-protocol.md, Unread summary), and takes no reactions, replies, pins, reports, edits or unsending.

## Editing and unsending

A member can change or take back their own messages, at any time. Others' messages cannot be changed.

- **編集** (text messages only): the message's words go back into the composer in place of all that was written there, as Signal and LINE put the line alone in it: the words, the line answered, the days brought to share, the photos picked and a link's page taken off with × go, and the line's words come with their mentions as @name again, under a bar saying メッセージを編集 with × to stop, which lets them go and leaves the composer empty. It stops a reply, as 返信 stops editing: the composer answers a line or changes one, never both, with one bar for it (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, composer). The send button becomes ✓ and the photo and day tools are hidden: only the words change. Saving replaces the words for everyone and marks the message 編集済み, shown over its time (on one line with a pin, when it is pinned). A message cannot be saved empty; taking it back is 送信取消. The link's page stays while its first link does; a new first link gets a new preview (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, edited).
- **送信取消** (any of one's own messages: words, photos, shared days): asked first in a centered alert (送信を取り消しますか？ / メンバー全員のチャットから消えます。, or in the chat with Pochical's people ポチカルを作っている人のチャットからも消えます。 / キャンセル | 取り消す). The message's content and reactions are removed for everyone; in its place a line in the middle says 〇〇がメッセージの送信を取り消しました (メッセージの送信を取り消しました for one's own). A reply that quoted it shows 取り消されたメッセージ, and the chat list's last line says the same as the line.
- In the message's menu, 編集 comes after the other actions and 送信取消 last, apart and in the danger color.
- Neither sends a notification, and neither changes unread counts. Both go through the change log as edits (spec/sync-protocol.md).

## Replies

- **返信** is the first item in every line's menu, one's own and others', words, photos, shared days and polls alike. It stops editing, the line's words going with the edit and the composer empty under the quote; else what was written stays, and what goes with it. It quotes the line over the composer (〇〇に返信, the line in one line of words, its first photo small beside it, × 返信をやめる, which leaves what was written) and goes to the composer (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, composer). The quote goes with the first line sent then (shared days, then the photos, then the words), and is gone from the composer.
- A line is also answered by dragging it sideways, as Signal, Telegram, LINE and Messages answer one: toward the middle from its writer's side (others' lines to the right, one's own to the left), the line following the finger and the reply arrow showing in the room it leaves, growing in as it goes. At `chatRules.replySwipe` points the arrow is whole and a haptic marks it (spec/haptics.md); past it the line follows the finger less, as a rubber band. Let go there, the line springs back and it is answered exactly as 返信 does; let go short of it, the line springs back and nothing happens. With motion reduced the line goes back at once, without the spring. Only a drag mostly across starts it, so a scroll a little aslant stays a scroll, and it never opens what the finger went down on (a link, a mention, a photo); a long press still opens the menu. Only lines whose menu has 返信 are dragged: not the app's lines (who joined or left, a line taken back), a blocked member's line folded away, or a line still on its way or not sent. On the phones going back by a drag from the screen's left edge stays the system's; iOS's going back by a drag anywhere on the screen gives way on a line that would be dragged to the right, as in Messages, and works as ever elsewhere.
- A reply keeps the line it answers by its place in the chat (`reply_to`, the seq): the group keeps it only for a line of the same chat the writer can see, not taken back, and else takes the line without it, as when the line was taken back meanwhile. Taking the reply back drops it.
- In the chat, a reply's words or photos sit in a bubble under the quote: the writer's name and the line in one line of words, in the bubble's own color, over a thin rule across it; shared days and polls show none. A reply starts a run. A tap on the quote goes to the line, asking for earlier lines until the chat's lines reach it, and lights it, as a pin does. A long press on it is the reply's own, opening its reactions and menu as on its words, as LINE and iMessage take a press anywhere on the bubble; the finger lifting from it goes nowhere.
- The quote reads as the line does in a line of words (quotes, below), 取り消されたメッセージ once taken back, and ブロック中のメンバーのメッセージ for a blocked member's. The group sends the line a reply answers with the reply, however far back it is (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat), so the quote reads as the line even before the chat is scrolled back to it; it says 以前のメッセージ only until that line has come.
- A reply notifies and counts as any line does.

## Reactions

- Any member reacts to any line (not one taken back) with an emoji, from the bar over it on a long press: 👍 ❤️ 😂 👀 🙏 🎉, the reader's own on the accent's container, then + for any other, from the system's emoji keyboard (Opening a message's menu). Several emoji may be on a line, and one member may choose several.
- Under the line, each emoji sits with the faces of who chose it, in the order they did, past three two faces and +N, in a pill on the card's ground; the reader's own on the accent's container, edged in the accent. In a chat of two (a 1対1 chat, the chat with Pochical's people) who chose it needs no face: the emoji alone, and 2 beside it once both did. A tap puts yours on or takes it back; a long press lists everyone who chose it by name.
- Each emoji keeps the place it came on the line in, after those already there, for as long as anyone holds it: the one who chose it first taking theirs back moves it nowhere, so a device putting its member's own on or off shows what the group will keep (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, reactions). One nobody holds any more goes, and chosen again it comes last, as a new one. Taking a line back (送信取消) takes its reactions off. A reaction sends no notification and changes no unread count.
- Reactions coming while the chat is open pop in where they go, as Telegram's and iMessage's tapbacks land: the line grows to make room for its first, in the same motion as any line growing (`chatRules.lineMoveMs`), and the pill pops in under the bubble in the accent from the moment it comes, growing as the line does; another emoji pops in the same way beside the others. The reader's own choice, from the bar or by a tap on a pill to join it, also sprays its emoji up from the pill, over the lines above, with its haptic; taking one's own back sprays nothing. Others' reactions only pop. The pop and the spray play in the line's own place: while they play, the line draws past itself, over the lines next to it (under the pins and the composer), and draws within itself again once they have played out, as at rest a line growing shows what it gains as it grows, never over the next one first; a first reaction's pill, which plays as its line grows, may show over the next line for that moment. While a line's menu is open, what comes to the lines plays under the menu's dimming, the open line's included; one picked from the bar pops and sprays at the pick, as the menu goes, under the line's lifted bubble until it has gone back to its place. VoiceOver never reads the pop or the spray. A pill that goes shrinks and fades. Reactions there as the chat opens, or as earlier lines come in, are simply there.
- A reaction is the member's edit of the line (`ChatReact`, on or off), which the group takes only for one emoji (spec/text-limits.md, isEmoji). The line comes again with every reaction on it, so a device simply shows the latest.

## Reporting and blocking

The stores require a way to report what people post and to block someone (App Store Review Guideline 1.2; Google Play's user-generated content policy). Neither is shown to the member concerned, and neither changes anything for the rest of the group.

- **通報** is in the menu of someone else's message (last, apart and in the danger color) and in the ⋯ menu of their profile sheet. A sheet asks the reason, one of 迷惑・スパム / 嫌がらせ・いじめ / 性的・暴力的な内容 / なりすまし / その他, and sends it with ✓ (on Android with 送信 at the sheet's foot, under the reasons as Material's radio buttons list them, as a Material sheet confirms; the sheet opens whole for it to be in sight). Then a centered alert says 通報しました and offers to block them too (〇〇をブロックしますか？… / しない | ブロック), unless they are already blocked; otherwise the app says 通報しました. The sheet says what is sent, and nothing else of the chat is: 通報すると、このメッセージと前後の数件がポチカルに送られます。 (Pochical's people read it on the admin site and in their Slack channel, spec/admin.md.) (for a member: 〇〇の名前とアイコン) 相手には知らされません。 It does not say who reads it, so it does not read as the chat being watched.
- **ブロック** is in the ⋯ menu of someone's profile sheet (beside × on iOS, at the sheet's top end on Android, whose sheets close by a swipe or back rather than ×; not in sight under their face: it is rarely used, and a family member's profile should not show it in red), first, with 通報 after a line in the danger color, asked first (〇〇をブロックしますか？). While blocked, their profile says ブロック中 under the name, and the menu has ブロックを解除. It applies to the account, in every group the two share:
  - their messages in group chats are folded to one line, ブロック中のメンバーのメッセージ, which shows the message for now on a tap; the chat list's last line says the same;
  - their one-to-one chat with you is hidden, cannot be started, and their messages to it are not delivered;
  - their shifts still show: the group exists to share shifts, and leaving the group or taking them out is the step for that.
- A block is undone with ブロックを解除 on their profile, or in 設定 › 通知 › チャット's ブロック中のメンバー (shown while there is one), asked first.
- The server also filters what is posted for known abusive material before it reaches the group; what it catches is not delivered.
- A report made by or about someone who deletes their account is kept a year after the deletion, then erased with its Slack message (spec/sync-protocol.md, Reports after a deletion). A line taken back, or the app's notice, is not reported.

## Unread lines and typing

- A chat opened with unread lines opens on the first of them, under a line saying ここから新着 (a rule either side, in the accent); the app's own lines, the reader's, and a line never delivered to the reader (from someone they blocked, in a one-to-one chat) are not counted (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, writer and firstUnread), here and in every count of unread lines: ↓'s, the chat list's and the group's. The line stays where it is while the chat is open and is gone the next time it opens. A chat opened from a shared day (the shift table, the landing page) opens on that day instead. With nothing unread, it opens on the latest line. The chat opens at once on the lines the device holds, as the messaging apps do, never blank while the group's catch-up is still coming (or offline): which lines are unread, and so where it opens, is chosen from those, and lines the catch-up brings after come in under them as any new line does. The lines show once where they open, so ここから新着 is the first thing seen, not a trip from the latest; with few new lines, ここから新着 is in sight at the latest already and the chat opens there. Whether the chat follows its latest line is the person's: it stops as they scroll, and starts again where their scroll comes to rest at the foot, as they send, or at ↓. While following, others' lines, someone writing, the keyboard or the composer growing, and anything else changing the lines' height (a photo or a link's page drawn) keep it at the foot; others' unread lines coming in at once, more than the screen shows (as the catch-up brings them after the chat opened), never carry it past the first of them (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, firstComing): it stops there under ここから新着, as Telegram and Slack do, unless the chat has that line already. 続きを読む on a line opens it where it is, its words going on down, and stops following, as the messaging apps open a long one. Scrolled up, it stays where it is, and the ↓ counts the new lines. One's own line just sent at the foot comes in with the lines sliding up to it, rising from under their foot as it fades in, as the messaging apps send; others' lines and someone writing come in at the foot the same way, and with motion reduced each fades in where it goes, the lines put there at once (Reduced motion). Lines are read as they come on the screen, not as they arrive, as Signal and Telegram read them: the reader's mark goes on to the latest line whose foot is on the screen, as the person scrolls down through unread lines too, not only at the latest; once the lines have stayed a moment (so a fling reads once where it stops), never back, and only while the lines are shown and the app is in front (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, readTo). Lines come in below the screen stay unread until they are scrolled to. A chat lays out at most its latest page (`chatRules.pageSize`) as it opens, back to the reader's first unread line when that is earlier, up to `chatRules.openMax` lines (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, openingFrom); lines that come while it is open add to those. Once the chat has opened, the lines laid out stay where they begin as later lines come past a gap in those the device holds, as the catch-up after a while away brings only the latest page: they go on to the last of those held without a gap after them, never swapped for the later ones (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, window). While they stop short of the latest line held, the device asks the server for the page straight after them once their foot is `chatRules.prefetchScreens` screens' height away, measured where the lines are after each scroll and each change as for the earlier lines below, once for each last line laid out (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, pageAfter), again each time the socket opens after a drop, since the answer may have gone with it, and they go on into it as lines coming in do, stopping under ここから新着 as the catch-up's do, and on to the latest once it meets the lines held after. Meanwhile ↓ counts others' lines after them as unread, each line not held yet among them, and shows at their foot too; a tap lays out the latest lines as the chat opens and goes there, stopping under ここから新着 at others' unread lines among them, the lines shown, as the chat opens, only once there, so where they first land is neither seen nor read; one's own line sent lays them out the same way and goes on to their foot, where that line is, as it does at the latest. A jump to a line after them (a pin, a quote) lays out from that line. Earlier lines come a page at a time before the person reaches the top of the lines: the next is laid out once the top is `chatRules.prefetchScreens` screens' height away, from the lines the device holds, and the device asks the server for the page before those it holds whenever it holds fewer than a page before the lines laid out (again each time the socket opens after a drop), so scrolling back waits at the top only when it outruns the server, a spinner there meanwhile. They never come in as the chat opens, which would add lines above the latest it opens on. Whatever changes the lines, the lines seen keep their place on the screen through it, held at the very top included: a page coming in above (pages one after another, as a fast fling brings them), a line growing or shrinking above them (a reaction, a link's page, an edit, a long line's 続きを読む measured), a line unsent. Only following moves them, to the foot, and whether the chat is at its foot is told from where it was before each change, not after it. A line growing or shrinking where it is (a reaction, an edit, a link's page come later, 続きを読む) moves to its new height with the lines around it, in the same motion as lines coming in at the foot (`chatRules.lineMoveMs`): its foot stays and the lines over it go up or down with it, as the messaging apps grow a line, so the lines under it never move; everything of a line moves as one with its bubble (the face, the name, the time, 編集済み, the pin and the reactions), never a part ahead of the rest; what it gains shows as it grows, never drawn over the line under it first. 続きを読む keeps its top instead, its words going on down, and a chat following its latest keeps its foot. While the person scrolls (not as the chat follows its latest), the day of the lines at the top floats over them, fading in as the scrolling starts and out `chatRules.floatingDayMs` after it stops. It is kept as a list pins its section's header (iOS's Settings, Telegram): a day's title takes it over once the title's words are up to where it floats, the title itself then not drawn under it, nor once the floating day has faded (Telegram's), and on the way there the title coming up under the day pushes it up and out, the two never drawn over each other, so the day shown changes only once the one before has gone. The push goes with the lines as they move, the finger's or a fling's, never as a motion of its own. Lines shorter than the screen sit at its foot with nowhere to scroll.
- Scrolled up more than half the screen from the latest line, with unread lines below not yet seen, or with the lines laid out stopping short of the latest (read or not), a round ↓ shows at the foot of the lines; a tap scrolls to the latest. While ここから新着 is below the top of the screen (scrolled up over it) and lines under it are still unread, a tap first brings it to the top, as Signal goes, and the next goes on to the latest; it follows the latest again only once at the foot. With motion reduced, ↓ and a jump put the lines where they go at once, never gliding. While others' lines are unread, the ↓ carries their count in the unread badge's red, as LINE and Slack count what is below, going down as they come on screen and are read; a line once on screen stays read.
- A count of unread lines stands for what notifies: every unread line of a chat that is on, and in a chat turned off only those that mention the reader, while メンションはいつも通知 is on (`spec/vectors/unread.json`). A group's icon in the list of groups shows its chats' count, and the グループ tab all groups'. Each chat in a group's chat list still shows all its own unread lines. The 設定 tab counts the answers from Pochical's people not read yet, which a chat on its row in settings also shows; opening that chat reads them.
- Someone writing shows as three dots rising in turn, in a bubble of the others' kind with their picture, under the latest line (from the typing frames in spec/sync-protocol.md). A screen reader hears 〇〇が入力中. A blocked member's typing is not shown. Nobody sees whether you have read their lines.

## Pins

- Any member can pin a line (words, photos, shared days) from its menu, in a group chat or a one-to-one chat, and anyone can take a pin off (ピン留めを外す). It is the same for everyone in the chat. No line from the app says who pinned it.
- At most `chatRules.maxPins` lines are pinned at once, as LINE keeps five announcements; one more takes the place of the oldest, and the app says so. Pinning a line already pinned only moves it up (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, pins).
- Under the header, a card shows the latest pinned line (glass on iOS, the lines scrolling on under it as under the header's buttons), floating `chatRules.pinMargin` from the screen's sides and from the header alike: a pin, ピン留め (ピン留め・N件 with more than one) and its words on one line. A tap jumps to the line, in the middle of the screen, and lights it once there, as Telegram lights a message it goes to: the accent over its bubble inside the bubble's outline, tail and all (a card's or large emoji's own rounded ground), coming and going once over `chatRules.jumpFlashMs`, with one band of shine sweeping across it, never past the outline. It goes there gliding, over the last screen only from further away rather than through every line between, as ↓ goes to the latest; VoiceOver reads on from it. With more than one, ▾ opens all of them under the bar, the latest first, each with who wrote it, inside the card: it grows to hold them and they show only within it as it opens and closes.
- A long press (or a right click) on the bar, or on a line of its list, offers ピン留めを外す, as a line's long press opens its menu. There is no × on the bar: a pin is everyone's, so taking it off is not left a stray tap away.
- A pinned line has a small pin by its time. Taking a line back (送信取消) takes its pin off too.

## Polls

A group chat can put days to the vote, as LINE's 日程調整 does, for the step after finding days everyone is off.

- みんなのシフト's day sheet rises as tall as its bar and a row a member, at the reader's text size, every member's row in sight, and grows to the whole screen when pulled up; while it is up the list behind keeps that much room at its foot, so a day near the end can be scrolled above it. Another day tapped while it is up, in any layout, takes the open one's place in the same sheet at the height it is at, as the calendar's day does; the sheet does not go down and come up again. It has 全体チャットで共有 at its top: it opens 全体チャット with the day set over the composer (共有する日, the day in one line, × 共有をやめる), as sharing into a chat app does; nothing reaches everyone until it is sent, with words if one likes, the day going first.
- A poll is started from the composer's ＋, whose 日にちの投票 sits beside 日にちを共有 in a group chat (one-to-one chats have no polls), as LINE's and Telegram's attach menus offer a poll of their own. Both open the same day sheet, in the way it was opened, which nothing inside it changes: titled 日にちを共有 or 日にちの投票. Putting days to the vote, ✓ waits until `POLL_MIN_DAYS` days are picked (design/src/limits.ts; the note asks for 候補の日を〇日以上), and then says 〇日の中から、みんなが行ける日を投票で決めます。; ✓ sends a poll instead of the days. The days sheet has no other way in: みんなのシフト's 全体チャットで共有 puts its day in the composer to be shared (共有する日).
- The poll is a card: a head (日にちの投票, and N人が投票) whose long press opens the line's reactions and menu like any line's, then a row per day: the date (in the week's colors), みんな休み under it when everyone's shifts are off, the faces of who can come (three, or two and +N), and a 行ける button that toggles your vote. Anyone in the group votes, on as many days as they like, and can change it until the poll is settled.
- Its writer has 日にちを決める at the card's foot (anyone has it once the writer has left the group, so a poll is never stuck): a sheet lists the days with how many can come; ✓ settles it. The chosen row is marked 決定 on the accent's container, the other days fade, voting ends, and the poll is pinned (Pins) so the day stays found. The app says 〇月〇日(〇)に決めました to the writer. A settled poll's foot is gone, so the day reads as decided; whoever can settle it finds 決め直す in its long-press menu, opening the same sheet on the day chosen. Choosing another moves 決定 to it, and the pin bar follows, for everyone; no line from the app says so. ✓ waits while the day already chosen is picked, as it would change nothing.
- A tap on a day's faces lists everyone who can come that day by name, as a reaction's list does.
- In a line of words (quotes, the chat list, the pin bar) a poll reads 📅 日にちの投票：〇月〇日(〇)ほか, and once settled 📅 〇月〇日(〇)に決定. The chat list says what a line did: shared days and a poll still open end with を共有しました (📅 10月5日(日)ほかを共有しました); a settled poll keeps its day.

## Photos

- The composer is drawn as iOS 26's Messages draws its own, over the lines' foot rather than a bar across the screen, the lines scrolling on under it: a round ＋ (写真や日にちを送る), and beside it a capsule holding what the message carries (the bars for 返信, 編集, 共有する日 and a link's page, then the photos picked), the field, and the send button at its end; it grows from one line to a rounded box as they come. ＋ opens a small menu from itself, as Messages' ＋ opens its apps: 写真, which opens the photo picker, then 日にちを共有, and in a group chat 日にちの投票, in that order whichever way it opens. The support chat, with the photo tool alone, has the photo tool itself (写真を送る) in ＋'s place. While a message is being changed (編集), the round is gone and the capsule takes the row. The @ list of names to mention floats over the composer on its own. On iOS the round, the capsule, the @ list and the menu are the system's glass; on Android each is Material's own surface in the same places, but for the composer's row, which stands under the lines on a surface of its own rather than over their foot, as Material's messaging apps (Google Messages) keep it: with no glass to see the lines through, lines under it would only be hidden.
- The send button is each platform's own, as its messaging apps draw it: the arrow up in a round at the capsule's end on iOS (Messages), Material's send on Android; grey and not pressable while there is nothing to send.
- The composer's photo tool opens the system's photo picker, `chatRules.photosPerSend` photos at most; past them it says 写真は一度に{n}枚まで送れます, and a photo the phone cannot read is left out with 開けない写真がありました. Picked photos wait above the composer, 64pt squares each with × ({n}枚目の写真を外す), until sent; words written with them go after them as a line of their own.
- Images pasted into the composer's field (the edit menu's ペースト, the keyboard's paste, ⌘V) go above it as picked photos do, in the same tray and by the same rules: as many as it has room for under `chatRules.photosPerSend`, saying 写真は一度に{n}枚まで送れます past it, and 開けない写真がありました for one the phone cannot read. Words pasted go into the field as ever; an image copied with words pastes the image. While a message is being changed (編集) an image pastes nothing, as its photo tool is hidden then. The system's own paste is used, so the edit menu offers ペースト for an image and pasting asks nothing more.
- The photos sent at once are one line, with no words (Albums). The phone shrinks each first, as chat apps send them: its longer side to `chatRules.photoMaxEdge` pixels, as JPEG within `chatRules.photoMaxBytes` (what is see-through in it, as a sticker a keyboard puts in, laid on white, as a JPEG has none), turned upright and written again from its pixels, so its location and other metadata never leave the phone. When its longer side is past `chatRules.photoSmallEdge`, the phone also sends a small version shrunk to that (`PUT …/photos/{id}?size=small`, before the photo), for the chat's lines and quotes to fetch in its place (`GET …?size=small`, which gives the photo itself while it has none); opened large and saved, it is the photo itself. Shrinking on the phone rather than on the server costs nothing and leaves nothing to wait for. From the photo's JPEG, drawn at most 100 pixels on its longer side, the phone makes the photo's ThumbHash (https://evanw.github.io/thumbhash/, at most `chatRules.photoThumbHashMaxBytes` bytes; `spec/vectors/thumbhash.json`), sent with its size; the server keeps it beside the photo and hands it on as it came, refusing a longer one. A photo that comes without one (one sent before it, or one whose ThumbHash could not be made) is shown as before.
- In the chat a photo has no bubble (one answering a line sits in a bubble under its quote, a little in from its edge, keeping its own corners and edge): rounded at `lg` with a hairline edge, the size it was sent at within 220×260 points, cropped at its ends when wider than 2:1 or taller than 1:2. While it comes over the network its place shows the blur its ThumbHash draws, stretched to the photo's shape, and the photo fades in over it in `chatRules.photoFadeMs` milliseconds once it comes; opened large, the blur shows whole on black the same way. One that cannot be fetched (offline, or the server failing) keeps its blur, or its grey, now still, with ↻ in a dark circle over it (写真をもう一度読み込む), as Signal and Telegram mark one: a tap anywhere on it fetches it again, rather than opening it, and a long press on it still opens its line's reactions and menu; opened large, ↻ shows in the middle the same way. A photo without a ThumbHash shows a grey that breathes instead (fading to 40% and back every 1.6 seconds, still when motion is reduced or it cannot be fetched), opened large the system's spinner on black, and shows at once when it comes. While it uploads it is dimmed, a ring in a dark circle filling as its bytes go (its small version's and its own as one), turning a quarter while it waits its turn or for the network, as Signal and Telegram ring one; × in the ring (送信をやめる) stops its line, and the line is then not sent (Lines on their way) as if it had failed. Once every photo of the line is up, the line is being sent and can no longer be stopped: the ring stays full without ×, the line's clock telling it is on its way as a line of words being sent does, until the line is taken. A × tapped just as it comes up either stops the line or does nothing, never marking a line not sent that goes anyway. A tap opens it large (below), but not while it uploads.
- The phone fetches a photo once however many places ask for it at once (its line, a quote, the photo opened large), reads it straight at the size it shows (at most the screen's pixels for its box), and keeps it two ways, as image libraries do: drawn at that size in memory, `chatRules.photoMemoryBytes` at most, so a line scrolled back draws at once; and as fetched on its disk among its caches, `chatRules.photoDiskBytes` at most, the least lately read let go first past it, so a photo seen once comes without the network. A photo whose line is taken back is let go from it at once, and every photo with the account as it is deleted or left; a group left lets go of its lines' photos and the member's own kept for it, and a group's lines reset (a `Reset`, spec/sync-protocol.md) let go of the photos the device held of them, since a line taken back while the device was away comes again only as taken back. The member's own photos, held on the phone as sent, and the faces the widgets show, are kept apart from that limit. Faces (members' and groups' photos) and links' page pictures come the same way, read at the size they show and kept drawn in memory beside the photos, so a line drawn again (the chat opened again, a line scrolled back) shows them at once rather than a face's letter first; on the disk each is kept once, where the phone already keeps it. While the chat scrolls, the photos of the lines within `chatRules.photoPrefetchScreens` screens above and below those in sight are fetched ahead, and those scrolled further away are let go before they come, as Signal and Telegram fetch what is about to show. A line of photos has 保存 (all its photos) and ピン留め in its menu, and 送信取消 for its sender; no コピー or 編集. Saving tries every photo, one that fails not stopping the rest, and says 写真を保存しました, or 一部の写真を保存できませんでした when some could not be saved and 保存できませんでした when none could; the photo opened large saves the one shown, saying the same.
- A photo opened large is shown as Photos, Signal and Telegram show one: whole on black, with × at the top, its place among all the chat's photos (2 / 5), as Telegram, Signal and LINE count them, beside it while there are more than one, and 共有 (the system's share sheet, with the photo itself) and 保存 at the bottom; someone's face opened from their profile has × alone. Swiping sideways goes to the chat's photos before and after it, one by one in the order of its lines, a line's in the order it holds them, all the way to its first and its latest, the lines laid out or not: those the reader sees (none of a line taken back, nor, in the place's own chat, of a member they blocked, whose lines fold away; one shown for once opens alone, as does one of the reader's still on its way). Its photos come at once from the lines laid out, and the rest from the group a page at a time (`chatRules.photoPageSize`, spec/sync-protocol.md Chat) once fewer than `chatRules.photoPageAhead` are left on a side, the count showing once the group has said where it is, and a page asked again should the socket drop before it comes; the two photos on each side are fetched ahead as it shows. The count is only ever the group's (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat): while it is open, a photo sent in the chat, taken back or hidden, or someone blocked, has the group asked again where the one shown is, the count staying as it was meanwhile; one taken back drops out at once, the next shown in its place should it be the one shown, and it closes once none is left; a new one is swiped to at the end once the group's pages reach it (`spec/vectors/chat-photos.json`, run). Each shows what the chat drew of it at once (its thumbnail, else its blur, else the system's spinner) and the photo itself as it comes. Pinching or tapping twice zooms it (a double tap to twice its size about the point tapped and back out, a pinch up to four times, as Signal), dragging moves it about while zoomed, and a single tap hides or shows the buttons. It opens out of its place in its line and, closed with × or pulled down (the photo following the finger, the chat showing behind it), goes back into the place of the photo then shown, even one scrolled partly off the screen, that place left empty meanwhile, as Photos does, and filled again as the photo lands on it, fading into the photo there, never empty after it has landed; on iOS this is the system's zoom transition, whose pull is not taken while the photo is zoomed in. One whose line is not laid out on the screen (scrolled away, or from the group's pages alone) closes as the system closes it, with nothing to go back into. With motion reduced it fades in and out instead, and a pull down still follows the finger, the ground fading, and closes it pulled far enough or flicked.
- 写真 in the chat's ⋯ lists the chat's photos alone, as LINE's 写真・動画, WhatsApp's メディア and Messages' 詳細 › 写真 do: a page titled 写真, pushed over the chat, with a grid of square thumbnails four across from edge to edge, a hairline apart, the latest first (at the top left, reading across and down). They are the photos the photo opened large swipes through (those the reader sees, each photo of a line on its own) and come the same way: the latest page from the group as it opens (`chatRules.photoPageSize`, spec/sync-protocol.md Chat, asked from the latest), and the page before the oldest once fewer than `chatRules.photoPageAhead` are left under those that have shown, asked again should the socket drop before it comes; while it is open (its first page still on its way too) a new photo sent in the chat comes in at the top as the group's page around those in sight brings it, a page counted before it being asked again, and one taken back drops out at once (`spec/vectors/chat-photos.json`, run, from the latest). Each thumbnail is drawn as a line's photo is, cut to its square: its small version over its blur, else its grey breathing, ↻ on one that cannot be fetched; a screen reader reads whose it is and the day it was sent (〇〇が送った写真、10月5日). The system's spinner shows until the first page comes, and 写真はまだありません under a photo symbol, as the platform's empty pages say it, in a chat without any. A tap opens it large among the chat's photos (above), its count and swiping as from the chat, closing back into its square, that square left empty meanwhile. As it is swiped the grid scrolls under it to keep the square of the photo shown in sight, as Photos' grid follows, so it closes into that square.
- In a line of words (quotes, the pin bar) a line of photos reads 📷 写真, or 📷 写真{n}枚 for several; the chat list says 写真を送りました (自分：写真を送りました for one's own).
- Photos are kept by the server under their group, readable by its members alone through the server, never by a public address. A photo never changes once kept: no one, its sender included, can put other bytes in its place (the sender's phone uploading it again before sending its line again stores nothing), nor upload in place of a face or a group's mark. Taking a line of photos back deletes its photos, and their small versions, for everyone. A photo uploaded that no line came to carry (its line stopped or deleted before it went, or the app deleted or closed mid-send) is deleted once its uploader has uploaded nothing to the group for `chatRules.unsentAfterMs`; so each time the phone sends a line it uploads again, from the copy it keeps of each, the photos it has not seen go up within half of `chatRules.unsentAfterMs` (timed from when each began going up, so a line sent on them reaches the server well before it lets go of them), and a line sent again long after still finds them (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat).
- A line one of whose photos cannot be uploaded (refused, gone from the phone, or failing five tries a few seconds apart) is not sent, all of it (Lines on their way): its choices are 送れなかった写真's, もう一度送る uploading only the photos not yet up (as above), and 削除 takes the photos kept for it too.

## Albums

Photos sent at once are one line, as Signal sends them, its message holding all its pictures: the chat draws it as one album, and it is answered, saved, pinned, reacted to and taken back as one, as the person sees it. Telegram keeps an album's photos as lines of their own, answered and taken back one by one, which leaves the album's reactions on its first photo and has the album come apart as one is taken back.

- A line of two photos or more is drawn as an album; a line of one as a photo is. Pochical's people's photos posted at once from Slack are one line the same way, and a user's line of photos goes to their Slack thread as one message holding them all.
- An album is a grid with no bubble, as wide as a line with a link's page (240 points), rounded at `lg` with a hairline edge, its photos 2 points apart and each cropped to fill its cell. Its photos go in rows, top first: two side by side, three as one over two, four as two over two, and past that rows of two and three with the twos first (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, albumRows). A row of several is as tall as one of its cells is wide; the row of one, over two, is half the grid's width tall. Each photo shows its blur while it comes, as a photo does.
- The time sits beside the grid by its last photo, as for a photo, and a reply's quote over it in a bubble.
- A tap on one of its photos opens that photo large, the viewer going on through the chat's photos one by one as ever. A long press lifts the whole album with its line's menu, and its reactions are the line's, shown once under the grid. Dragging it sideways answers it.
- While its photos go up, each has its own ring filling as its bytes go, and × on any of them stops the line, all of it. Not sent, it has one ! and 送れませんでした, by its last photo.
- With a screen reader it is read as its line, its photos as how many (3枚の写真).

## Lines on their way

A line shows the moment it is sent, dimmed (as it would be on the chat's ground, so it looks the same lifted over the dimmed screen for its menu, and as the menu comes and goes), with a clock in its time's room, while it waits in the device's outbox for the group to take it (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat); it goes by itself whenever the group's socket is open, in the order written, as Signal and Telegram keep sending what waits.

- A line the device cannot put in its outbox (as on a device out of storage) does not show, and 保存できませんでした says so at once (spec/sync-protocol.md, Outbox): the composer keeps all it held, the words with their mentions, the line answered, the days brought to share and the photos picked, so 送信 can be pressed again; what went before it in the same send stays sent. Sharing days keeps its sheet open with the days picked. Any other edit of the person's in a chat (a reaction, a vote, a pin, a change, 送信取消, もう一度送る, 送信をやめる, a poll settled) is said not kept the same way, and nothing says it was done. A read mark is not the person's edit: one not kept is tried again as a line next comes into sight, its lines' notifications staying until then, and nothing is said.

- Where it shows, as iMessage, LINE and WhatsApp place one: a line still being sent comes after every other line, as the newest, so lines that come meanwhile go above it, and once the group takes it it is a line of the group's, in the group's order. A line not sent stays where it was written, among the others by its time (the time it was written against theirs, each by the server's clock), so a line that comes after goes under it, the latest. もう一度送る moves it to the end, as if written then. Lines not sent keep the order written among themselves, and lines still being sent keep it among theirs (spec/vectors/chat.json, order). A device shows a line not sent among the lines it has laid out only where it falls between them, and not past their end while it has later lines still to lay out, nor before their start while it has earlier ones.
- A line that has waited `chatRules.unsentAfterMs` since it was written without the group taking it (the phone offline all that time, as on a plane), one of whose photos cannot be uploaded (Photos), or that the group did not take (below), stops going by itself and is not sent from then on. The wait over is what makes it so, read from the line's time wherever it shows (the chat open or not, its socket open or not), never left for something to mark when it next runs: the chat list and the chat say so at the same moment, the moment it is over. Shown as not sent: a red ! beside it in its time's room and 送れませんでした under it, as Signal and Messages mark one. Words sent long after they were written may no longer be meant, so the person chooses, as Signal gives up on a message rather than sending it a day late. The lines after it go on. A tap on the ! opens a menu from it headed 送れなかったメッセージ (送れなかった写真 for a photo): もう一度送る sends it again, after the rest, as if written then; 削除 takes it away. A pick acts as the menu closes, not after it, as the system's menus act (an action sheet acts only once it has gone): the ! and 送れませんでした go and the line shows waiting, its clock and for a photo its ring, in the frame of the tap, though its upload may start later.
- The chat list shows a chat's latest line as the chat orders them (above): a line still being sent, else whichever comes last of the group's lines and the member's not sent, so a line from anyone after one not sent is the latest. A chat whose latest line is one of the member's not sent says so in place of 自分： and its words, as Messages and Telegram mark one in theirs: the line's red ! in the danger color, and 送れませんでした, which a screen reader hears. Its time stays the line's own. A draft still shows in its place (Drafts). Every chat's row says it alike: a group's, a one-to-one chat's and the chat with Pochical's people's.
- The wait is timed on the device's clock as the server corrects it (spec/sync-protocol.md, HLC), the line's time written the same way, so a clock changed while a line waits does not fail it early once a Welcome has corrected it. A line's time is never written again: a clock set back while offline keeps a line waiting until the clock passes it or a Welcome corrects the clock, as waiting longer offline does no harm, where moving its time would fail it early once corrected.
- The group says when it did not take a line, and the line is then not sent as above, with the same ! and 送れませんでした and the same choices, rather than going without a word: what the apps send fits, so this is only a bug's (what does not fit) or a one-to-one chat's whose other left the group just before (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat). A line to someone who blocked the sender is taken as any other (Reporting and blocking), so nothing tells a block.
- While it waits, a line's menu has コピー for words and 送信取消, asked first as for a sent line (Editing and unsending), the alert saying まだ送られていないメッセージです。 instead; 削除 of a line not sent is not asked, as nobody has it. Either takes it out of the outbox, and should it have gone out on the socket the group may have taken it meanwhile (sent just before, its answer not yet come, or lost with a socket that died): the device then takes it back from the group by the send's op_id (`ChatRecall`, spec/sync-protocol.md Chat), which takes the line back as 送信取消 does a sent line if the group has it, and else keeps its send from coming after; a line of photos names its photos, which the server deletes either way, so none is left uploaded with no line to show it. One that never went (written offline, or its photos never all up) the group cannot have, and nothing is sent for it; its photos already up the server lets go of by itself (Photos).

## Drafts

What is being written in a chat stays with the chat, as Signal and Telegram keep it.

- The words with their mentions, the line being answered and the line being changed are kept on the device as they are written, once the writing pauses for `chatRules.draftSettleMs`, and at once as the composer empties, the chat is left or the app leaves the screen, so the app ending however it does, even while the chat is open, never brings back words sent and loses at most the moment of writing before it. They are in the composer again as the chat opens. Photos picked, days brought to share and a link's page taken off with × are not kept. A draft is the device's own: not sent to the group, nor to the member's other devices. A draft the device cannot keep (as on a device out of storage) is tried again as the words next change, and nothing is said while the composer still holds them; as the chat is left or the app leaves the screen, when they would go, 保存できませんでした says so (spec/sync-protocol.md, Outbox), once the chat has gone or the app is back.
- Only words make a draft: a quote alone or words of nothing but spaces are not kept, nor an edit whose words are still the line's own. Sending it, or emptying the composer, ends it.
- Opened again: a reply whose line was taken back meanwhile keeps its words without the quote, as the group would take it then (Replies); an edit whose line was taken back, or is no longer on the device, goes with its words, which were that line's (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, draft).
- In the chat list, a chat with a draft says 下書き： in the danger color and the draft's words in a line, in place of its latest line, as Telegram marks one; its time and unread count stay those of its latest line, and it keeps its place. A one-to-one chat started and left with only a draft stays in the list with it. Leaving the group takes its drafts with it.

## Screen readers

What a finger does on a chat by a gesture, VoiceOver (TalkBack) does by the line's own actions, as Messages and Signal offer them; the group chats and the chat with Pochical's people alike, as they are one chat's lines.

- Each line is one element, read in the order the eye takes it: who wrote it (自分 for one's own, the writer's name on every line, not only a run's first), the line it answers (〇〇「…」への返信), its words (a mention as @name; a photo by its shape, 横長の写真, 縦長の写真, 正方形の写真 or パノラマの写真, as nothing sent with it says more, and photos sent together as how many, 3枚の写真; shared days and a poll as their summary), a link's page by its title, its time, then how it stands: ピン留め中, 編集済み, 送信中 while it waits, 送れませんでした once it stopped. Its reactions close it, summed up as each emoji and how many chose it (自分も when the reader did); who chose each is the line's more content (その他のコンテンツ), not read with it. The writer's face and name over a run are not read apart from the line.
- Its actions, in this order: もう一度送る and 削除 on a line not sent, 送信をやめる on photos going up; 写真を開く on photos, the first of several; every item of its menu as the long press opens it, 返信 first, as the swipe does; リアクション, which opens the reactions and menu over the chat for the screen reader to go into; each reaction on it put on or taken off (👍を付ける, 👍を外す); 返信元を表示 for a reply; リンクを開く with the link's site (one for each link, two to one site included), and 〇〇のプロフィール for each mention; the writer's profile; シフト表で見る. Activating it opens a photo large, else its menu, as the long press does.
- A card of shared days or a poll keeps its days and 行ける to be gone through one by one; the line is read first, by its time, carrying the same words and actions.
- The days' titles and ここから新着 are headings, for the screen reader's rotor to go from day to day. Scrolling a page at a time says the day of the lines at the top, as the floating day shows it to the eye (最新のメッセージ at the foot), which itself is not read. After a jump (a pin, a quote, ↓ to ここから新着) the screen reader reads on from the line gone to.
- Others' lines coming in while the chat is open are said wherever it is scrolled, once they have settled for `chatRules.announceSettleMs`: one by who and what (the app's notices by their words), several as 新しいメッセージ{n}件 and the latest, so a run coming at once is said once rather than each cutting the last off; queued after what the screen reader is saying, and not for one's own lines or a blocked member's.
- The bars in the composer (返信, 編集, 共有する日) are each read as one, what they are and what they hold, their × apart.
- A photo opened large is read as whose it is, its shape and when it was sent, and its place among the chat's photos (5枚中2枚目) while there are more than one. Swiping by three fingers goes to the photos before and after it, saying the one come to and its place; its actions 拡大 (twice its size, up to four times) and 元の大きさに戻す zoom it, and three fingers then move about it. The scrub (two fingers in a Z) closes it, and a double tap on it never hides its buttons.

## Reduced motion

With the system's 視差効果を減らす (Reduce Motion) on, nothing in a chat moves that a finger is not moving: what slides, grows or springs fades instead, in place, as Apple asks of it, rather than being left out. The same in the group chats and the chat with Pochical's people.

- A line's reactions and menu fade in and out where they are: the bar and the menu are not grown from the bubble's corner, the bubble is not lifted, nothing springs, and a reaction under the held finger is lit rather than grown. A bubble the menu has to move or draw smaller (a tall line, the keyboard up) is put there at once, not slid.
- A line sent, others' lines and someone writing fade in at the foot, the lines put there at once rather than sliding up; ↓, a jump and following the latest put the lines where they go at once, never gliding (Unread lines and typing). 続きを読む opens a line at once.
- A line growing or shrinking where it is (a reaction, an edit, a link's page) takes its new height at once, the lines around it put there with it. A reaction coming or going fades in or out where it is, without its pop or spray.
- A line jumped to is lit, still, for the same `chatRules.jumpFlashMs`, without the light coming and going or the shine's sweep.
- A line let go after a drag to reply goes straight back, without its spring; the drag itself follows the finger as ever.
- The composer's bars and photos come and go by fading, the capsule growing and shrinking with them. The lines and ↓ make room with them as they come and go, and with the keyboard, which the system moves as ever: they never stand apart from what moves under them, which would open a gap over the keyboard as it rises.
- The floating day and ↓ fade, as they always do; the next day's title pushes the floating day out as ever, since it goes with the lines rather than moving by itself. A notice over the screen (コピーしました) fades without coming down from the top.
- A photo opened large fades in and out instead of the zoom out of its place (Photos); a photo coming and its upload ring stay still: no breathing grey, no turning quarter, the ring still filling as its bytes go.
- Haptics are not motion and stay as they are, but a reaction's goes with its spray (spec/haptics.md).

## Text size and contrast

- Everything in a chat follows the reader's text size, the accessibility sizes included, without cutting anything off: the words and the times, and what is drawn around them, which grows with them rather than staying at its points: the reactions' pills and faces, the unread count's badge, ↓, a line's reactions and menu (its rows, its width and the reactions' rounds, within the screen's width, an item's words shrinking to stay on one line rather than being cut, and a menu taller than the room scrolled through, every item still reachable), a card of shared days or a poll (its small dates, names, faces, table and 行ける, as far as `chatRules.cardsMostScale`, so a week or six people still fit across a line), and the composer's ＋ and send, which with ↓ grow only as far as `chatRules.controlsMostScale` so the field and the lines keep the room. At the accessibility sizes a line takes nearly the screen's width, keeping 16 points rather than 40 on the other side, and a line with a link's page is as wide as that rather than its 240 points, as Messages gives its bubbles the width. A photo keeps its box (Photos), already nearly as wide as a line may be on the smaller phones, and a bubble's corners and tail keep theirs: they hold no words.
- With the system's 文字を太くする (Bold Text) the words are bold wherever the system's type is used, which is everywhere in a chat, and the lines are measured again for it.
- With コントラストを上げる (Increase Contrast) every screen, a chat's included, draws its quieter roles in a stronger one's color (`design/src/colors.ts`, increasedContrast): times, names and placeholders in the secondary words' color, hairlines and separators as strong borders, others' bubbles a step off the ground, and the accent's quiet border (ここから新着, a line rung) in the accent itself, in every テーマ, light and dark. The web follows prefers-contrast the same way.

## Long messages and shared days

A message may be as long as `textLimits.chatMessage` and share up to `SHARED_DAYS_MAX` days (`design/src/limits.ts`), but the chat keeps either from filling the screen.

- A message's words longer than `chatRules.foldLines` lines are cut at the last with `…`, and 続きを読む under them, in the color the bubble's links take, opens the rest in place, as LINE's 全文表示 does. Whether words run past it is measured as they are laid out, not guessed from their length. Opened stays opened while the chat is open.
- A card of shared days shows `chatRules.dayCardRows` of them, a week, in rows (or `chatRules.dayCardColumns` across when its people do not fit across, as it turns), and ほか{n}日 under them; シフト表で見る under the card shows them all.
- Choosing days to share stops at `SHARED_DAYS_MAX`: a day past it stays unpicked and a problem toast says 一度に送れるのは{n}日までです. The server refuses a message with more.

## The chat with Pochical's people

設定 has a chat with the people who make Pochical (作っている人とチャット), over ポチカルについて: a small wish or trouble is easier written in a chat than a mail, and the answer comes back in the same place. Its row is drawn as a chat in a group's chats' list is: the app's icon, the latest line (or 下書き： and the draft left in it; ほしい機能や不具合のこと、気軽にどうぞ while there is none) and the answers not read yet. Its head, over the first line once the lines reach it, says who it reaches and everything that does: what is written there and its photos, and the app's version and the device, sent with each line.

- It is a chat of its own place (Where a chat is kept), kept as a group's chats are: the Group DO `support:{userId}`, its members the user and Pochical's people (`chatRules.pochicalMember`), who answer under the app's name and icon, its one chat thread "group" (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat). The device keeps it by the place id `chatRules.supportPlace`, in the tables a group's chats are kept in. So everything a group chat does it does the same way, by the same code: its lines kept on the device and opened at once, paged a page at a time, sent through the outbox (Lines on their way), read as they come on the screen, notified and answered on the notification (Notifications), its drafts (Drafts), photos (Photos), link pages (Previews), reactions, replies and 送信取消.
- What differs is its place's rules, `chatPlaces.support`: no shared days, polls, pins, mentions, reports or one-to-one chats, and no 編集, as an answer may already be written to the words; and each line goes with the app's version and the device (`ChatSend.device`).
- Pochical's people's lines show with the app's icon and open no profile; the title is ポチカル, with no count under it. A tap on a notification of their answer opens the chat over 設定, as its row there does.
- The socket to its place is open while 設定's row or the chat is on screen, as a group's while its page is.
- Pochical's people answer from the admin pages and from Slack (spec/admin.md), as a member writes: the user's devices with the chat open take it as any line, and the rest by its count and notification. The notification is titled 作っている人とチャット (`SUPPORT_TITLE`), with the answer's words and no writer, and left a plain notification rather than a message from a person, so it reads as their chat's answer, not as a notice from the app; it is answered on, opened, stacked, taken back and turned off as any chat's.
