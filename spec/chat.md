# Chat

How chat messages behave beyond sync (spec/sync-protocol.md): which words are links and mentions, how a link's preview is made, when a mention notifies, editing and unsending, and reporting and blocking. Both native apps and the server follow it. The web prototype's `textParts` in `apps/web/src/lib/chat-text.ts` (tested in `apps/web/tests/chat-text.test.ts`) and the chat in `/design` follow this spec.

The numbers named `chatRules.*` here are in `design/src/chat.ts`, the one place they are written; `mise run gen` gives the apps the same values.

## Where a chat is kept

A chat is kept in a place: a group, whose chats are 全体チャット and its one-to-one chats, or a user's chat with Pochical's people. Every place keeps its chats alike, in a Group DO (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat), so a chat's lines, pages, outbox, read marks, notifications and photos work the same in each; what a place's chats can do differs only by its rules, `chatPlaces` in `design/src/chat.ts`, which the server and both apps read:

- `directChats`: any two members have a one-to-one chat beside the place's own; without it the place's only chat is its own (thread "group").
- `days`: lines share days (日にちを共有), `polls`: days are put to the vote, `pins`: lines are pinned, `edit`: words are changed after sending (編集), `mentions`: words mention a member, `reports`: lines and members are reported and blocked.
- `sendsDevice`: each line goes with the app's version and the device (`ChatSend.device`), kept for whoever reads the chat, as its head says.

The server drops an edit its place does not take (one in a one-to-one chat, a line of days or a poll, 編集, a pin, a vote or settling a poll) and refuses a report; the apps leave each out of their menus and tools. Within a group, what a one-to-one chat lacks (polls, mentions) is the thread's, not the place's.

## The chat's page

- The title is the group's name for 全体チャット, with how many are in it under it, or the other member's name for a one-to-one chat. A tap on it opens what the chat is of: the group's settings, or the member's profile. While the group's socket is not open (接続中…) or its catch-up is still coming (更新中…, spec/sync-protocol.md, Keepalive) for longer than `socketRules.statusShownAfterMs`, that is said under the title instead, as LINE, Telegram and WhatsApp say it, so lines shown are not taken for all there are; it goes as soon as the socket is current.
- A member's profile, from their face, their mention or a one-to-one chat's title, is a sheet: their face, which opens large when it is a photo, their name, ブロック中 while blocked, 〇〇でのプロフィール with the group's mark, and メッセージを送る for the one-to-one chat with them, but in that chat itself and while they are blocked. ブロック and 通報 are in its ⋯ (Reporting and blocking).
- A one-to-one chat with someone who left the group, or was taken out of it, stays in the hub's chats to be read, but takes no more lines: 〇〇はグループを抜けました stands where the composer was (相手のアカウントは削除されました once their account is deleted, spec/sync-protocol.md).
- ⋯ beside the title holds みんなのシフト for 全体チャット, then the chat's notifications (Mentions).
- In the group's hub, under its chats, a group of one says メンバーを招待すると、1対1でも話せます。 Starting a one-to-one chat lists the members with 〇〇での名前とアイコンで話します。, as the chat belongs to the group.
- コピー says コピーしました.

## Opening a message's menu

A long press on a message (a right click on the web) opens its reactions and menu, as LINE, WhatsApp and iMessage do: the rest of the screen dims, the message stays where it was, lifted a little, with the reactions in a bar over it and the menu under it, or over the bar when there is no room under it, as LINE turns it; only when neither fits are they all moved together to stay on the screen (a message too tall for the room drawn smaller about its top corner by the writer, as the system's context menu draws a tall preview, down to half its size and past that cut short at its end), and the message's place in the chat left empty meanwhile. A keyboard up stays up as it opens, as LINE keeps it, when the message, its bar and its menu fit over it with the message where it was; typing there closes the menu. When they do not (a small screen, a tall message), the keyboard goes down for the room, as iMessage puts it down. Should the chat move under it meanwhile (the keyboard going down, lines coming in), the message, its bar and its menu go along with it. A tap outside closes it; a pick acts as it closes, as the system's context menu does, so what it opens (the keyboard for 返信) comes in while the menu goes, quick at first and settling, rather than after it. On the phones the finger that opened it may stay down and slide onto a reaction or a menu item, which lights up under it with a light tick; lifting there picks it, but only once the finger has slid (as far as the system's long press lets a finger wander), never when the menu came to a still finger, and lifting anywhere else leaves it open for a tap, as the system's context menu does. The apps draw it themselves rather than the system's context menu, which puts reactions inside the menu. The press gives a little under the finger from the moment it goes down; held `chatRules.longPressMs`, the menu opens and a light haptic marks it, the two together. iMessage plays its haptic first and opens the menu a beat later, as a message moved in that beat is dragged instead; a line here is never dragged, so nothing waits between them. A tap is the message's own: a link or a mention opens, a photo opens large, a shared day or a poll's head does nothing. So scrolling past a line never opens its menu by accident. While the keyboard is up, a tap off the messages (beside or between them) puts it away, as LINE does, and so does dragging the lines down with it; a tap on a message stays the message's own. A long press on a link opens the link's menu instead. With a keyboard or a screen reader, activating the message opens its menu (a photo's activation opens it large; its menu is in the screen reader's actions).

## What is a link

- A link starts with `http://` or `https://` (any case) and runs over the characters a URL is written in (ASCII letters, digits and `-._~:/?#[]@!$&'()*+,;=%`). Any other character ends it, so Japanese written right after a link is not part of it: in `https://example.jp/ここどう？` the link is `https://example.jp/`.
- `.,!?:;'*` at its end belong to the sentence, not the link. So does a closing `)` or `]` with no opening one inside the link (`(https://example.jp)` → `https://example.jp`; `https://example.jp/A_(B)` keeps its bracket).
- A link needs a host; `https://` alone is not one.
- Addresses without a scheme (`example.jp`, `www.example.jp`), mail addresses and phone numbers are not links. Both apps use this rule instead of the platform's own detectors (`NSDataDetector`, `Linkify`), which find different things, so a message has the same links on every phone.

## In a message

- A message's bubble is round all over (`lg`), but the first of a run of one writer's (and a reply, a photo answering a line, and someone writing's dots): a tail at its top corner by the writer's side (top right for one's own, top left for others'), by their face and name, where LINE puts one, so it says whose words follow. It is one outline, never a tail laid on a corner: drawn for one's own W×H with r = `lg`, the top edge runs into the corner's arc, which goes down to a notch at x = W − 0.25r; from there a horn rises (a cubic, controls notch + (0.2r, −0.25r) and (W + 0.25r, 0.04r)) to its tip level with the top edge at (W + 0.6r, 0), and curves back in, concave (controls (W + 0.3r, 0.12r) and (W, 0.55r)), to the side at (W, min(1.5r, H − r)); the other corners are plain arcs, and others' bubbles are its mirror. The tail reaches past the bubble's frame: by the face every line of a run keeps 10 points for it, so the lines stay in line and it never touches the face; by the screen's edge it reaches into the margin, as LINE's and Messages' do. The same outline holds what the bubble holds (a link's page is cut to it), rings it when a line is jumped to, and is the bubble the long press lifts. Others' bubbles are on the left, one's own on the right. The face and the name stay at the top of the run. A run's lines sit close (`chatRules.lineGap`), and more room comes before a run's first line, a day's title and ここから新着 (`chatRules.runGap`), so each turn reads as one, as iMessage and LINE space them. The words are at body size, as Messages draws them. A line's time shows beside it, but for a line the next goes on the run of, sent in the same minute: that minute shows once, by the last of them, as LINE does. A line without its time keeps the time's room (and one being sent, its clock in that room), so a bubble is as wide either way and never widens as the next line comes. 編集済み and the pin stay on each line.
- Links are underlined; in others' messages in the accent color, in one's own in the bubble's text color.
- A tap on a link opens it in the system's browser sheet over the chat (`SFSafariViewController`, Custom Tabs).
- A long press on a link (or a right click) opens the link's own small menu, リンクを開く and リンクをコピー, as iOS offers on a link in text, without the reactions: on the web under the link, in the apps where the line's menu goes. It opens held as long as the line's menu (`chatRules.longPressMs`), and lifting the finger then opens nothing. The message's menu stays shorter without a link item.

## Large emoji

A message of nothing but 1 to `chatRules.largeEmojiMax` emoji shows them large and without a bubble, as iMessage does: drawn at `chatRules.largeEmojiSize` (points on iOS, sp on Android) at the reader's default text size, growing with it as body does. Each one is an emoji as a mark's is (spec/text-limits.md, `isEmoji`); a space, a line break or any other character among them keeps the message an ordinary bubble, and so does a reply, as the line it answers sits inside its bubble. `spec/vectors/chat-text.json` (largeEmoji) pins which messages count.

- Only how the message is drawn changes: it is sent and kept as words like any other, so an edit that leaves only emoji, or adds words to them, redraws it at once. The chat list's last line, a quote of it and its notification show it at the usual size.
- The time sits beside it, its reactions under it and its long press opens its reactions and menu, as on a bubble; a jump to it rings the emoji.

## Previews

A message carries at most one preview, for its first link. It is made while the message is written and sent with it, or by the server just after, so everyone sees the same preview and receivers never fetch the page.

- While writing, once the first link has stayed the same for `chatRules.linkPreviewSettleMs`, the app asks the server for its preview and shows it above the composer: the site's name, the title (読み込み中… until it arrives) and the picture small at the end, with × to send without it. × holds for that link until the message is sent or the link changes.
- A message sent before its preview arrives goes without one, and the server reads the page itself once it has the line, adding it to the line for everyone as if it had been sent with it; unless × took it off (`ChatSend.without_preview`), or the line is taken back or its first link changed meanwhile. If the server finds no page, nothing is shown above the composer, and the line stays without one.
- In the chat, the preview sits inside the message's bubble, under its words: the picture (cropped to `chatRules.linkPreviewAspect`, width over height), the title (two lines at most) and the site's name. A tap opens the link; a long press opens the message's reactions and menu.

## Pochical's invitation links

A link to `https://pochical.app/invite/{code}` (the host in any case, an optional trailing `/`, query and fragment ignored; `inviteCodeOf` in `apps/web/src/lib/chat-text.ts`) is one of Pochical's own invitations. The chat treats it as part of the app, not as a page:

- A tap on it, in the words or on its card, opens the group's join screen in the app, as reading its QR code does; when you are in the group already, it opens the group. Nothing opens in the browser, and nothing joins until you confirm on the join screen.
- Its card shows the group instead of a page: the group's mark at the hub's size on a tint, its name, and グループへの招待・{n}人 (参加中のグループ once you are in it). A long press opens the message's actions, as on a page's card.
- The card is not made while writing or sent with the message. Each app asks the server as the message shows (`GroupService.GetInvite`, which also says whether the reader is in the group), so a link that was remade or whose group was deleted turns into この招待は使えません for everyone, and a renamed group shows its new name. That card opens nothing; a tap on the link's words says the link cannot be used.
- Nothing is shown above the composer for it, and it takes the place of the message's one preview: a message whose first link is an invitation shows no page for later links.

## Reading a page

The server reads the page, so that people's addresses are not sent to the sites, and so both apps get the same result.

- The title is the page's `og:title`, else its `<title>`; the site's name is `og:site_name`, else the host without `www.`; the picture is `og:image`. A page with neither title gives its address without the scheme as the title.
- Only `http` and `https` on ports 80 and 443, to hosts that resolve to public addresses (no private, loopback or link-local ranges, checked again after each redirect). At most 3 redirects, 5 seconds, and the first 512 KB of HTML.
- The picture is fetched by the server (an image of at most 2 MB, as the site serves it) and stored with the preview, served to signed-in users from Pochical's own storage (`GET /v1/previews/{id}`), so it keeps showing after the site changes or removes it. A larger picture is left out.
- The server asks `ChatService.GetLinkPreview` with the link; the preview goes with the message (`ChatSend.preview`), and an edit says whether its first link stayed (`ChatChange.keeps_preview`) or gives the new link's page.
- Previews are cached by URL for a day.

## Mentions

A mention names one member of the group in a group chat (全体チャット). One-to-one chats have none.

- A message keeps a mention as `<@id>`, the member's id in the group, and shows it as @ and their name in the group as it is when read, so a later name change shows the new name. Copying a message, a chat's last line in the list, a quote and a notification show the same @name as plain words. A member who has left keeps their last name.
- While writing, an `@` (or the full-width `＠` a Japanese keyboard types) at the end of the message lists the other current members whose name contains what follows it (`spec/vectors/chat-text.json`, mentionQuery). Picking one writes `@name` and a space; only those picked become mentions when the message is sent, and only while `@name` is still followed by a space or the end. Typing a name by hand mentions no one.
- In a message, a mention is in the name's weight, in others' messages in the accent color; one of the reader looks the same, as the chat list's @ is what finds it. A tap on a mention opens that member's profile, except the reader's own.
- There is no mention of everyone: a group chat's line already reaches everyone, and a member who turned it off chose quiet.

### Notifications

- A group chat turned off sends no notifications, except for a line that mentions the reader, which notifies as if the chat were on. The chat notification settings say so under the group chats' switches, and so does a group's own 通知.
- Each chat's notifications turn off and on in its menu (通知をオフにする, 通知をオンにする, saying so as it changes); a group's chat also on 設定 › 通知 › チャット and in the group's own 通知. That page lists the one-to-one chats turned off as it opens, to turn back on, and those turned on again stay until it is left. A chat turned off shows a crossed-out bell after its name in the chat list and its title. Turning a chat on asks for the system's permission first, if not yet asked, and the page shows a card while notifications are not allowed: to ask, or once refused, to open the system's settings.
- メンションはいつも通知 (設定 › 通知 › チャット › メンション, on by default) turns that exception off: off, a chat turned off sends nothing, mentions included, and the notes are not shown. It is one switch for the account, kept by the User DO, not one per group.
- In the chat list, a chat whose unread lines mention the reader shows @ in the accent color before the unread count.
- A line's notification is a message from its writer, as Messages, Signal and Telegram show one: the writer's face and name as the group shows them (their photo once the device has it, else their first letter, as everywhere), and for 全体チャット the group's name and mark as the conversation's, its words then worded as a one-to-one chat's (`CHAT_{…}`), as the system shows who wrote them already. A device that does not hold the group yet shows it as the server words it. A chat's notifications stack together, and a tap opens the chat at ここから新着, as from the chat list.
- A line's notification is answered on it (返信), without opening the app, as in LINE and Messages: the words go as a new line of the reader's in that chat, not quoting the line, through the same outbox as the chat's composer, and the chat is read up to that line, the app icon's badge then following the counts as they come. Nothing is sent for empty words. The device waits until it is sent while the system gives it time; what has not gone by then goes when the app next opens. An answer from Pochical's people has no 返信.
- Once lines are read, their notifications go from the device and from the user's other devices, as in Signal and Telegram: those of the chat's lines at or before where the reader read to (spec/vectors/chat.json, withdrawn). The device that read takes them back at once; the others are told by the server (spec/sync-protocol.md, Push), and, should that not reach them, take back what they know is read as their counts come: lines at or before the reader's own read mark they hold, or a chat whose count comes to nothing unread (a count the device held from before it opened may be older than the notifications since, so it is not one).
- A chat turned off is the same: its notifications, a mention's while メンションはいつも通知 is on, look, are answered and go the same way.

## Notices

全体チャット says, as a line from the app, what changes who is in the group, the way in, or what the group is, as LINE's groups do; nothing else. Someone joining (〇〇がグループに参加しました), leaving (〇〇がグループを抜けました, an account deleted while in the group too, named as deleted accounts are), being taken out by a member (〇〇が△△をグループから外しました), the invite link remade (〇〇が招待リンクを作り直しました: a link someone already sent stops working, and this tells them why and to send the new one), and a new name or icon (〇〇がグループ名を「…」に変更しました, 〇〇がグループのアイコンを変更しました, or both: 〇〇がグループ名を「…」にして、アイコンを変更しました). A member's own name or photo, shifts and the table's layout are not said: they show where they are, and would flood the chat.

- The Group DO writes it as the change is made, a `ChatLine` with `notice` (`ChatNotice`: the kind, who was taken out, the new name, whether the icon changed) and no words, its author being who did it, at the chat's next seq. Each device words it with the names the group shows now, those of members who left included.
- It sits centered in the tertiary color between the messages, as an unsent line does, and starts the run after it. The chat list says it as its last line.
- It is never unread and never notified (spec/sync-protocol.md, Unread summary), and takes no reactions, replies, pins, reports, edits or unsending.

## Editing and unsending

A member can change or take back their own messages, at any time. Others' messages cannot be changed.

- **編集** (text messages only): the message's words go back into the composer, its mentions as @name again, under a bar saying メッセージを編集 with × to stop. The send button becomes ✓ and the photo and day tools are hidden: only the words change. Saving replaces the words for everyone and marks the message 編集済み, shown over its time (on one line with a pin, when it is pinned). A message cannot be saved empty; taking it back is 送信取消. The link's page stays while its first link does; a new first link gets a new preview (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, edited).
- **送信取消** (any of one's own messages: words, photos, shared days): asked first in a centered alert (送信を取り消しますか？ / メンバー全員のチャットから消えます。 / キャンセル | 取り消す). The message's content and reactions are removed for everyone; in its place a line in the middle says 〇〇がメッセージの送信を取り消しました (メッセージの送信を取り消しました for one's own). A reply that quoted it shows 取り消されたメッセージ, and the chat list's last line says the same as the line.
- In the message's menu, 編集 comes after the other actions and 送信取消 last, apart and in the danger color.
- Neither sends a notification, and neither changes unread counts. Both go through the change log as edits (spec/sync-protocol.md).

## Replies

- **返信** is the first item in every line's menu, one's own and others', words, photos, shared days and polls alike. It stops editing, quotes the line over the composer (〇〇に返信, the line in one line of words, a photo small beside it, × 返信をやめる) and goes to the composer. The quote goes with the first line sent then (shared days, then each photo, then the words), and is gone from the composer.
- A line is also answered by dragging it sideways, as Signal, Telegram, LINE and Messages answer one: toward the middle from its writer's side (others' lines to the right, one's own to the left), the line following the finger and the reply arrow showing in the room it leaves, growing in as it goes. At `chatRules.replySwipe` points the arrow is whole and a haptic marks it (spec/haptics.md); past it the line follows the finger less, as a rubber band. Let go there, the line springs back and it is answered exactly as 返信 does; let go short of it, the line springs back and nothing happens. With motion reduced the line goes back at once, without the spring. Only a drag mostly across starts it, so a scroll a little aslant stays a scroll, and it never opens what the finger went down on (a link, a mention, a photo); a long press still opens the menu. Only lines whose menu has 返信 are dragged: not the app's lines (who joined or left, a line taken back), a blocked member's line folded away, or a line still on its way or not sent. On the phones going back by a drag from the screen's left edge stays the system's; iOS's going back by a drag anywhere on the screen gives way on a line that would be dragged to the right, as in Messages, and works as ever elsewhere.
- A reply keeps the line it answers by its place in the chat (`reply_to`, the seq): the group keeps it only for a line of the same chat the writer can see, not taken back, and else takes the line without it, as when the line was taken back meanwhile. Taking the reply back drops it.
- In the chat, a reply's words or photo sit in a bubble under the quote: the writer's name and the line in one line of words, in the bubble's own color, over a thin rule across it; shared days and polls show none. A reply starts a run. A tap on the quote goes to the line, asking for earlier lines until the chat's lines reach it, and rings it, as a pin does. A long press on it is the reply's own, opening its reactions and menu as on its words, as LINE and iMessage take a press anywhere on the bubble; the finger lifting from it goes nowhere.
- The quote reads as the line does in a line of words (quotes, below), 取り消されたメッセージ once taken back, and ブロック中のメンバーのメッセージ for a blocked member's. The group sends the line a reply answers with the reply, however far back it is (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat), so the quote reads as the line even before the chat is scrolled back to it; it says 以前のメッセージ only until that line has come.
- A reply notifies and counts as any line does.

## Reactions

- Any member reacts to any line (not one taken back) with an emoji, from the bar over it on a long press: 👍 ❤️ 😂 👀 🙏 🎉, the reader's own on the accent's container, then + for any other, from the system's emoji keyboard (Opening a message's menu). Several emoji may be on a line, and one member may choose several.
- Under the line, each emoji sits with the faces of who chose it, in the order they did, past three two faces and +N, in a pill on the card's ground; the reader's own on the accent's container, edged in the accent. In a chat of two (a 1対1 chat, the chat with Pochical's people) who chose it needs no face: the emoji alone, and 2 beside it once both did. A tap puts yours on or takes it back; a long press lists everyone who chose it by name.
- Emoji keep the order they were first chosen in, and one nobody holds any more goes. Taking a line back (送信取消) takes its reactions off. A reaction sends no notification and changes no unread count.
- A reaction is the member's edit of the line (`ChatReact`, on or off), which the group takes only for one emoji (spec/text-limits.md, isEmoji). The line comes again with every reaction on it, so a device simply shows the latest.

## Reporting and blocking

The stores require a way to report what people post and to block someone (App Store Review Guideline 1.2; Google Play's user-generated content policy). Neither is shown to the member concerned, and neither changes anything for the rest of the group.

- **通報** is in the menu of someone else's message (last, apart and in the danger color) and in the ⋯ menu of their profile sheet. A sheet asks the reason, one of 迷惑・スパム / 嫌がらせ・いじめ / 性的・暴力的な内容 / なりすまし / その他, and sends it with ✓. Then a centered alert says 通報しました and offers to block them too (〇〇をブロックしますか？… / しない | ブロック), unless they are already blocked; otherwise the app says 通報しました. The sheet says what is sent, and nothing else of the chat is: 通報すると、このメッセージと前後の数件がポチカルに送られます。 (Pochical's people read it on the admin site and in their Slack channel, spec/admin.md.) (for a member: 〇〇の名前とアイコン) 相手には知らされません。 It does not say who reads it, so it does not read as the chat being watched.
- **ブロック** is in the ⋯ menu of someone's profile sheet (beside ×, not in sight under their face: it is rarely used, and a family member's profile should not show it in red), first, with 通報 after a line in the danger color, asked first (〇〇をブロックしますか？). While blocked, their profile says ブロック中 under the name, and the menu has ブロックを解除. It applies to the account, in every group the two share:
  - their messages in group chats are folded to one line, ブロック中のメンバーのメッセージ, which shows the message for now on a tap; the chat list's last line says the same;
  - their one-to-one chat with you is hidden, cannot be started, and their messages to it are not delivered;
  - their shifts still show: the group exists to share shifts, and leaving the group or taking them out is the step for that.
- A block is undone with ブロックを解除 on their profile, or in 設定 › チャット › ブロック中のメンバー (shown while there is one), asked first.
- The server also filters what is posted for known abusive material before it reaches the group; what it catches is not delivered.

## Unread lines and typing

- A chat opened with unread lines opens on the first of them, under a line saying ここから新着 (a rule either side, in the accent); the app's own lines and the reader's are not counted (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, firstUnread). The line stays where it is while the chat is open and is gone the next time it opens. A chat opened from a shared day (the shift table, the landing page) opens on that day instead. With nothing unread, it opens on the latest line. The chat opens at once on the lines the device holds, as the messaging apps do, never blank while the group's catch-up is still coming (or offline): which lines are unread, and so where it opens, is chosen from those, and lines the catch-up brings after come in under them as any new line does. The lines show once where they open, so ここから新着 is the first thing seen, not a trip from the latest; with few new lines, ここから新着 is in sight at the latest already and the chat opens there. Whether the chat follows its latest line is the person's: it stops as they scroll, and starts again where their scroll comes to rest at the foot, as they send, or at ↓. While following, others' lines, someone writing, the keyboard or the composer growing, and anything else changing the lines' height (a photo or a link's page drawn) keep it at the foot; others' unread lines coming in at once, more than the screen shows (as the catch-up brings them after the chat opened), never carry it past the first of them (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, firstComing): it stops there under ここから新着, as Telegram and Slack do, unless the chat has that line already. 続きを読む on a line opens it where it is, its words going on down, and stops following, as the messaging apps open a long one. Scrolled up, it stays where it is, and the ↓ counts the new lines. One's own line just sent at the foot comes in with the lines sliding up to it, rising from under their foot as it fades in, as the messaging apps send; others' lines and someone writing come in at the foot the same way, and with motion reduced each shows at once. Lines are read as they come on the screen, not as they arrive, as Signal and Telegram read them: the reader's mark goes on to the latest line whose foot is on the screen, as the person scrolls down through unread lines too, not only at the latest; once the lines have stayed a moment (so a fling reads once where it stops), never back, and only while the lines are shown and the app is in front (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, readTo). Lines come in below the screen stay unread until they are scrolled to. A chat lays out at most its latest page (`chatRules.pageSize`) as it opens, back to the reader's first unread line when that is earlier, up to `chatRules.openMax` lines (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, openingFrom); lines that come while it is open add to those. Earlier lines come a page at a time before the person reaches the top of the lines: the next is laid out once the top is `chatRules.prefetchScreens` screens' height away, from the lines the device holds, and the device asks the server for the page before those it holds whenever it holds fewer than a page before the lines laid out, so scrolling back waits at the top only when it outruns the server, a spinner there meanwhile. They never come in as the chat opens, which would add lines above the latest it opens on. Whatever changes the lines, the lines seen keep their place on the screen through it, held at the very top included: a page coming in above (pages one after another, as a fast fling brings them), a line growing or shrinking above them (a reaction, a link's page, an edit, a long line's 続きを読む measured), a line unsent. Only following moves them, to the foot, and whether the chat is at its foot is told from where it was before each change, not after it. While the person scrolls (not as the chat follows its latest), the day of the lines at the top floats over them once its own title has gone up out of sight, as LINE shows it, and goes `chatRules.floatingDayMs` after the scrolling stops. Lines shorter than the screen sit at its foot with nowhere to scroll.
- Scrolled up more than half the screen from the latest line, or with unread lines below not yet seen, a round ↓ shows at the foot of the lines; a tap scrolls to the latest. While ここから新着 is below the top of the screen (scrolled up over it) and lines under it are still unread, a tap first brings it to the top, as Signal goes, and the next goes on to the latest; it follows the latest again only once at the foot. With motion reduced, ↓ and a jump put the lines where they go at once, never gliding. While others' lines are unread, the ↓ carries their count in the unread badge's red, as LINE and Slack count what is below, going down as they come on screen and are read; a line once on screen stays read.
- A count of unread lines stands for what notifies: every unread line of a chat that is on, and in a chat turned off only those that mention the reader, while メンションはいつも通知 is on (`spec/vectors/unread.json`). A group's icon in the list of groups shows its chats' count, and the グループ tab all groups'. Each chat in a group's chat list still shows all its own unread lines. The 設定 tab counts the answers from Pochical's people not read yet, which a chat on its row in settings also shows; opening that chat reads them.
- Someone writing shows as three dots rising in turn, in a bubble of the others' kind with their picture, under the latest line (from the typing frames in spec/sync-protocol.md). A screen reader hears 〇〇が入力中. A blocked member's typing is not shown. Nobody sees whether you have read their lines.

## Pins

- Any member can pin a line (words, a photo, shared days) from its menu, in a group chat or a one-to-one chat, and anyone can take a pin off (ピン留めを外す). It is the same for everyone in the chat. No line from the app says who pinned it.
- At most `chatRules.maxPins` lines are pinned at once, as LINE keeps five announcements; one more takes the place of the oldest, and the app says so. Pinning a line already pinned only moves it up (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, pins).
- Under the header, a bar shows the latest pinned line: a pin, ピン留め (ピン留め・N件 with more than one) and its words on one line. A tap jumps to the line and rings it, in the middle of the screen: gliding there, over the last screen only from further away rather than through every line between, as ↓ goes to the latest; VoiceOver reads on from it. With more than one, ▾ opens all of them under the bar, the latest first, each with who wrote it.
- A long press (or a right click) on the bar, or on a line of its list, offers ピン留めを外す, as a line's long press opens its menu. There is no × on the bar: a pin is everyone's, so taking it off is not left a stray tap away.
- A pinned line has a small pin by its time. Taking a line back (送信取消) takes its pin off too.

## Polls

A group chat can put days to the vote, as LINE's 日程調整 does, for the step after finding days everyone is off.

- みんなのシフト's day sheet has 全体チャットで共有 at its top: it opens 全体チャット with the day set over the composer (共有する日, the day in one line, × 共有をやめる), as sharing into a chat app does; nothing reaches everyone until it is sent, with words if one likes, the day going first.
- The day sheet (日にちを共有, from the composer's calendar tool) has 共有 | 投票 at its top in a group chat, on 共有 as it opens, so a poll is seen from the start; one-to-one chats have neither. On 投票 the title reads 日にちの投票, ✓ waits until two days are picked (the note asks for 候補の日を2日以上), and then says 〇日の中から、みんなが行ける日を投票で決めます。; ✓ sends a poll instead of the days. Both share one way in, as their sheet is the same.
- The poll is a card: a head (日にちの投票, and N人が投票) whose long press opens the line's reactions and menu like any line's, then a row per day: the date (in the week's colors), みんな休み under it when everyone's shifts are off, the faces of who can come (three, or two and +N), and a 行ける button that toggles your vote. Anyone in the group votes, on as many days as they like, and can change it until the poll is settled.
- Its writer has 日にちを決める at the card's foot (anyone has it once the writer has left the group, so a poll is never stuck): a sheet lists the days with how many can come; ✓ settles it. The chosen row is marked 決定 on the accent's container, the other days fade, voting ends, and the poll is pinned (Pins) so the day stays found. The app says 〇月〇日(〇)に決めました to the writer. A settled poll's foot is gone, so the day reads as decided; whoever can settle it finds 決め直す in its long-press menu, opening the same sheet on the day chosen. Choosing another moves 決定 to it, and the pin bar follows, for everyone; no line from the app says so. ✓ waits while the day already chosen is picked, as it would change nothing.
- A tap on a day's faces lists everyone who can come that day by name, as a reaction's list does.
- In a line of words (quotes, the chat list, the pin bar) a poll reads 📅 日にちの投票：〇月〇日(〇)ほか, and once settled 📅 〇月〇日(〇)に決定. The chat list says what a line did: shared days and a poll still open end with を共有しました (📅 10月5日(日)ほかを共有しました); a settled poll keeps its day.

## Photos

- While the composer's field is in use, from the moment it is tapped and while words wait in it, its tools (写真を送る, 日にちを共有) fold into a › (写真と日にちのボタンを表示), as LINE's do, giving the field their room; › opens them again until the next letter. The support chat, with the photo tool alone, keeps it in sight.
- The send button is each platform's own, as its messaging apps draw it: the arrow up on iOS (Messages), Material's send on Android; grey and not pressable while there is nothing to send.
- The composer's photo tool opens the system's photo picker, `chatRules.photosPerSend` photos at most; past them it says 写真は一度に{n}枚まで送れます, and a photo the phone cannot read is left out with 開けない写真がありました. Picked photos wait above the composer, 64pt squares each with × ({n}枚目の写真を外す), until sent; words written with them go after them as a line of their own.
- Images pasted into the composer's field (the edit menu's ペースト, the keyboard's paste, ⌘V) go above it as picked photos do, in the same tray and by the same rules: as many as it has room for under `chatRules.photosPerSend`, saying 写真は一度に{n}枚まで送れます past it, and 開けない写真がありました for one the phone cannot read. Words pasted go into the field as ever; an image copied with words pastes the image. While a message is being changed (編集) an image pastes nothing, as its photo tool is hidden then. The system's own paste is used, so the edit menu offers ペースト for an image and pasting asks nothing more.
- Each photo is its own line, with no words. The phone shrinks it first, as chat apps send them: its longer side to `chatRules.photoMaxEdge` pixels, as JPEG within `chatRules.photoMaxBytes`, turned upright and written again from its pixels, so its location and other metadata never leave the phone. When its longer side is past `chatRules.photoSmallEdge`, the phone also sends a small version shrunk to that (`PUT …/photos/{id}?size=small`, before the photo), for the chat's lines and quotes to fetch in its place (`GET …?size=small`, which gives the photo itself while it has none); opened large and saved, it is the photo itself. Shrinking on the phone rather than on the server costs nothing and leaves nothing to wait for. From the photo's JPEG, drawn at most 100 pixels on its longer side, the phone makes the photo's ThumbHash (https://evanw.github.io/thumbhash/, at most `chatRules.photoThumbHashMaxBytes` bytes; `spec/vectors/thumbhash.json`), sent with its size; the server keeps it beside the photo and hands it on as it came, refusing a longer one. A photo that comes without one (one sent before it, or one whose ThumbHash could not be made) is shown as before.
- In the chat a photo has no bubble (one answering a line sits in a bubble under its quote, a little in from its edge, keeping its own corners and edge): rounded at `lg` with a hairline edge, the size it was sent at within 220×260 points, cropped at its ends when wider than 2:1 or taller than 1:2. While it comes over the network its place shows the blur its ThumbHash draws, stretched to the photo's shape, and the photo fades in over it in `chatRules.photoFadeMs` milliseconds once it comes; opened large, the blur shows whole on black the same way. One that cannot be fetched (offline, or the server failing) keeps its blur, or its grey, now still, with ↻ in a dark circle over it (写真をもう一度読み込む), as Signal and Telegram mark one: a tap anywhere on it fetches it again, rather than opening it; opened large, ↻ shows in the middle the same way. A photo without a ThumbHash shows a grey that breathes instead (fading to 40% and back every 1.6 seconds, still when motion is reduced or it cannot be fetched), opened large the system's spinner on black, and shows at once when it comes. While it uploads it is dimmed, a ring in a dark circle filling as its bytes go (its small version's and its own as one), turning a quarter while it waits its turn or for the network, as Signal and Telegram ring one; × in the ring (送信をやめる) stops it, and its line is then not sent (Lines on their way) as if it had failed. A tap opens it large (below), but not while it uploads.
- The phone fetches a photo once however many places ask for it at once (its line, a quote, the photo opened large), reads it straight at the size it shows (at most the screen's pixels for its box), and keeps it two ways, as image libraries do: drawn at that size in memory, `chatRules.photoMemoryBytes` at most, so a line scrolled back draws at once; and as fetched on its disk among its caches, `chatRules.photoDiskBytes` at most, the least lately read let go first past it, so a photo seen once comes without the network. A photo whose line is taken back is let go from it at once, and every photo with the account as it is deleted or left. The member's own photos, held on the phone as sent, and the faces the widgets show, are kept apart from that limit. While the chat scrolls, the photos of the lines within `chatRules.photoPrefetchScreens` screens above and below those in sight are fetched ahead, and those scrolled further away are let go before they come, as Signal and Telegram fetch what is about to show. Its menu has 保存 and ピン留め, and 送信取消 for its sender; no コピー or 編集. Saving says 写真を保存しました, over the photo opened large too.
- A photo opened large is shown as Photos, Signal and Telegram show one: whole on black, with × at the top, its place among the chat's photos (2 / 5) beside it while there are more than one, and 共有 (the system's share sheet, with the photo itself) and 保存 at the bottom; someone's face opened from their profile has × alone. Swiping sideways goes to the chat's photos before and after it, in the order of its lines, as far as the lines the device holds (not a blocked member's folded away, nor one of the reader's still going up); each shows what the chat drew of it at once (its thumbnail, else its blur, else the system's spinner) and the photo itself as it comes. Pinching or tapping twice zooms it (a double tap to twice its size about the point tapped and back out, a pinch up to four times, as Signal), dragging moves it about while zoomed, and a single tap hides or shows the buttons. It opens out of its place in its line and, closed with × or pulled down (the photo following the finger, the chat showing behind it), goes back into the place of the photo then shown, even one scrolled partly off the screen, that place left empty meanwhile, as Photos does; on iOS this is the system's zoom transition, whose pull is not taken while the photo is zoomed in. One whose line is not on the screen any more closes as the system closes it. With motion reduced it fades in and out instead, and a pull down still follows the finger, the ground fading, and closes it pulled far enough or flicked.
- In a line of words (quotes, the pin bar) it reads 📷 写真; the chat list says 写真を送りました (自分：写真を送りました for one's own).
- Photos are kept by the server under their group, readable by its members alone through the server, never by a public address. Taking a photo's line back deletes the photo, and its small version, for everyone.
- A photo that cannot be uploaded (refused, gone from the phone, or failing five tries a few seconds apart) is a line not sent (Lines on their way): its choices are 送れなかった写真's, and 削除 takes the photo kept for it too.

## Lines on their way

A line shows the moment it is sent, dimmed (as it would be on the chat's ground, so it looks the same lifted over the dimmed screen for its menu, and as the menu comes and goes), with a clock in its time's room, while it waits in the device's outbox for the group to take it (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat); it goes by itself whenever the group's socket is open, in the order written, as Signal and Telegram keep sending what waits.

- A line that has waited `chatRules.unsentAfterMs` since it was written without the group taking it (the phone offline all that time, as on a plane), or whose photo cannot be uploaded (Photos), stops going by itself and is marked as not sent: a red ! beside it in its time's room and 送れませんでした under it, as Signal and Messages mark one. Words sent long after they were written may no longer be meant, so the person chooses, as Signal gives up on a message rather than sending it a day late. The lines after it go on. A tap on the ! opens 送れなかったメッセージ (送れなかった写真 for a photo): もう一度送る sends it again, after the rest, as if written then; 削除 takes it away.
- Failing is the device's alone. The group tells the sender of no refusal: what the apps send fits (a send that does not is dropped, spec/sync-protocol.md), and a line to someone who blocked the sender is taken as any other (Reporting and blocking), so nothing tells a block.
- While it waits, a line's menu has コピー for words and 送信取消, asked first as for a sent line (Editing and unsending), the alert saying まだ送られていないメッセージです。 instead; 削除 of a line not sent is not asked, as nobody has it. Either takes it out of the outbox, and should the group have taken it meanwhile (sent just before, its answer not yet come), the device takes it back from the group as it comes, as 送信取消 does a sent line.

## Drafts

What is being written in a chat stays with the chat, as Signal and Telegram keep it.

- The words with their mentions, the line being answered and the line being changed are kept on the device as the chat is left and as the app leaves the screen, and are in the composer again as the chat opens. Photos picked, days brought to share and a link's page taken off with × are not kept. A draft is the device's own: not sent to the group, nor to the member's other devices.
- Only words make a draft: a quote alone or words of nothing but spaces are not kept, nor an edit whose words are still the line's own. Sending it, or emptying the composer, ends it.
- Opened again: a reply whose line was taken back meanwhile keeps its words without the quote, as the group would take it then (Replies); an edit whose line was taken back, or is no longer on the device, goes with its words, which were that line's (`spec/vectors/chat.json`, draft).
- In the chat list, a chat with a draft says 下書き： in the danger color and the draft's words in a line, in place of its latest line, as Telegram marks one; its time and unread count stay those of its latest line, and it keeps its place. A one-to-one chat started and left with only a draft stays in the list with it. Leaving the group takes its drafts with it.

## Long messages and shared days

A message may be as long as `textLimits.chatMessage` and share up to `SHARED_DAYS_MAX` days (`design/src/limits.ts`), but the chat keeps either from filling the screen.

- A message's words longer than `chatRules.foldLines` lines are cut at the last with `…`, and 続きを読む under them, in the color the bubble's links take, opens the rest in place, as LINE's 全文表示 does. Whether words run past it is measured as they are laid out, not guessed from their length. Opened stays opened while the chat is open.
- A card of shared days shows `chatRules.dayCardRows` of them, a week, in rows (or `chatRules.dayCardColumns` across when its people do not fit across, as it turns), and ほか{n}日 under them; シフト表で見る under the card shows them all.
- Choosing days to share stops at `SHARED_DAYS_MAX`: a day past it stays unpicked and a problem toast says 一度に送れるのは{n}日までです. The server refuses a message with more.

## The chat with Pochical's people

設定 has a chat with the people who make Pochical (作っている人とチャット), over ポチカルについて: a small wish or trouble is easier written in a chat than a mail, and the answer comes back in the same place. Its row is drawn as a chat in the chats' list: the app's icon, the latest line (or 下書き： and the draft left in it) and the answers not read yet. Its head says who it reaches and everything that does: what is written there, and the app's version and the device, sent with each line.

- Each user's chat is their own, kept by the server (`proto/pochical/v1/support.proto`, D1's `support_messages` and `support_chats`), so Pochical's people can read every user's in one place. Pochical's people answer under the app's name and icon.
- It is moving into a place of its own (Where a chat is kept): the Group DO `support:{userId}`, a chat of two, the user and Pochical's people (member `chatRules.pochicalMember`, ポチカル), with `chatPlaces.support`'s rules, reached at `/v1/me/support/socket`. The server holds it already; the apps, the admin pages and Slack move onto it next, and what is said above and below of `SupportService` goes with that move.
- A line is the user's words, up to `textLimits.chatMessage`, sent with an id of the app's so a send tried again is kept once; past `SUPPORT_LIMIT` (wrangler.jsonc) it waits. A line that could not be sent is marked as a group chat's is (Lines on their way), at once rather than after a wait: the red ! and 送れませんでした, the ! opening もう一度送る, which sends it again after the rest, and 削除.
- Opening the chat reads its answers; until then they are counted on its row.
- What is being written stays with the chat as a group chat's does (Drafts): its words and the line being answered, kept on the device (the device's own, not sent to the server) as the chat is left and as the app leaves the screen, and in the composer again as it opens. Its lines are the server's, so the rule for a reply is taken once they come: a reply to a line taken back since, by the user or by Pochical's people, keeps its words without the quote, as a group chat's does; until they come (offline), it keeps its quote. 設定's row says 下書き： in the danger color and the draft's words in place of the latest line, its time and count staying the latest line's. - Pochical's people answer from the admin pages (spec/admin.md). An answer shows at once in the open chat and on 設定's row, and comes as a notification that opens the chat.
- Its lines are laid out as a group chat's: the day over each day's first line, runs by writer, and the time beside the last of a run's lines sent in the same minute. Its head, first, starts the chat at the top while the lines are few. It scrolls as a group chat does: it opens on the latest line, keeps to it as lines come, shows ↓ and the day floating over the lines, and slides a line sent up into place. The server gives its whole history at once, so no earlier lines are asked for as it is scrolled back.
- A line is held as a group chat's is (/design's SupportChatPage), for what fits a chat of two: reactions, 返信 and コピー, and on one's own lines 送信取消; not days, polls, mentions, pins or reports, and no 編集, as an answer may already be written to the words.

  - A reply quotes the line it is to (`reply_to`), gone to at a tap; a line is answered by dragging it sideways too, as a group chat's is (Replies); one to a line not in the chat is sent as a plain line.
  - A link's page shows as a group chat's line's does (Previews): read while writing and sent with the line (`SendSupportMessage.preview`), or read by the server as it keeps the line when it had not come, unless × took it off (`without_preview`); an answer of Pochical's people's gets its page from the server. The admin pages show its title and site under the words.
