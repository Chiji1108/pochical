# Admin site

Pochical's people read and answer users' chats with them, and look at what members reported, on a site of their own: `admin.pochical.app` (`apps/admin`). Nothing of it is in the apps: they would grow for everyone, and carry a way in for anyone who looked.

## How it reaches the data

- The site is its own Worker (React Router 8 in framework mode). Each page's loader, and its action for what it sends, run on the site's server, which asks Pochical's server through a service binding to the server's `AdminEntrypoint` (`apps/server/src/admin-entrypoint.ts`, Workers RPC). The entrypoint has no route: only a Worker bound to it reaches it, so nothing it does is open on the internet, and the server's public API has no admin part.
- What the entrypoint does is what the pages need and no more: list the support chats (from D1's index of them), read one a page at a time and answer it (through its Group DO, `support:{userId}`, spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat), list the reports, audit the photos bucket and delete what nothing names in it, and, before release only, erase everything (全消去).

## Who gets in

- Cloudflare Access stands in front of the whole Worker (its Access tab: Protect this Worker behind Access), so every way to it, `admin.pochical.app` and any other, asks Pochical's people to sign in first, by their email. Its `workers.dev` and preview addresses are off besides.
- The site checks nothing more itself: Access's `ctx.access` does not reach a Worker served behind its static assets, as React Router's is.
- Its actions answer only its own pages: React Router refuses a POST whose `Origin` is not the site's own (400) before any action runs, so a page elsewhere cannot send an answer or delete through someone signed in.
- Its forms work without JavaScript, as plain forms the server answers, and with it, without leaving the page.

## How it looks

- The site is drawn in ポチカル's own colors, the color roles of design/ for the ポチカル テーマ (`themeRoles`), light or dark as the computer is, with the Panda CSS of `@pochical/styled-system` as the web's other sites are, and its Japanese broken between phrases (`@pochical/jsx`).
- Its pages sit beside a sidebar of them by what they are for, 対応 (サポート, 通報, ユーザー) and 運用 (棚卸し, 全消去); on a narrow screen the sidebar is a bar along the top, whose links scroll sideways.

## What it holds

- **サポート**: every user's chat, the latest first, saying which wait for an answer (未返信, with how many of the user's lines since Pochical's people last wrote), which have lines waiting for Slack (Slack未送信, Slack), and the latest line. A chat shows its latest page of lines (`chatRules.pageSize`) oldest first, and 前のメッセージ for the page before, as the app pages it: each user's with the app version and device it came from, the line a reply is to, the photos (each opening whole), the emoji on each and whose, and the lines taken back; and a form to answer as ポチカル in words, its length counted in characters as a reader sees them (spec/text-limits.md) by the site as it is sent; the field does not stop typing, as the browser counts only UTF-16 units, and too long an answer comes back with its words and how many characters they are. Photos are read from the server's bucket through the AdminEntrypoint (`/photos/{user}/{photo}` on the site). An answer is a line of Pochical's people's in the chat's Group DO, told to the user as any line of a chat is: their open chat takes it on its socket, and their devices get its count and notification, which opens the chat. Only a chat the user opened takes one. Each page gives its form a draft id, and an answer's `op_id` (`answerSupport`) is made from the draft id and its words (SHA-256), so sending the same words again from the same form (pressed twice, or after an answer lost) is one line, answered as the first was, and other words are an answer of their own. An answer refused or not kept comes back with its words and its draft id; one kept loads the page again (Post/Redirect/Get), with a new draft.
- **通報**: what members reported, the latest first: when, why, the group, who reported, who was reported, and what they saw. A report keeps the line with the few around it, each with its writer's name in the group and its words with mentions as names, the reported one marked; or the member's name.

- **ユーザー**: every account (D1's `user`), the latest signed up first, each with how they sign in besides anonymously (Apple) and whether their chat with Pochical's people waits for an answer; a field finds them by the start of their id (`?q=`, a GET of the page, so with or without JavaScript), and one found alone opens their page. ⌘K (Ctrl+K), or the sidebar's ユーザーを探す, which the browser's invoker commands open without JavaScript, opens the same search from any page.
  - A user's page: when they signed up and how they sign in, the sessions signed in (one a device) and their push tokens, the groups they are in or have left with when (their User DO's memberships), their chat with Pochical's people, and what they reported and what members reported of them. Their User DO is asked only once D1 has their row, so a page asked for an id with no account makes none, and says it has none (404).
  - Each report's members, who reported and who was reported, link to their pages.

- **棚卸し**: the photos bucket (R2 `pochical-photos`) held up against what names each object in it (`bucketAudit`, `apps/server/src/bucket-audit.ts`), run as the page opens and again by 再実行. The audit only reads: nothing is deleted or written, so it may be run at any time and as often as wanted. Each prefix's objects are counted and weighed by verdict, with up to 20 keys of each, and the total nothing names over the table. Deleting what it finds is a step of its own, which Pochical's people decide on after reading it (削除, below).

  | Prefix | Written by | Named by | Verdicts when nothing names it |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `groups/{groupId}/photos/{photoId}` and `…/small` | a member's upload, Slack's images in a support chat, a copy of a user's own photo (`photos.ts`) | the Group DO (`support:{userId}` for a support chat): `group_photos` while unsent, shown, deleting or being taken back (the sweep owns those), a photos line's `chat_lines.content.photos`, the group's mark (`profile.photo`), a face (`members.usual_photo`, `own_photo`) | `unnoted` (no `group_photos` row), `takenBack` (its row says R2 holds it no more; only from before the sweep took back what lines leave), `sentUnnamed` (sent, but no line carries it: the lines Group DO migration 0035 emptied), `groupGone` (the group is not set up, or was erased, and nothing in it holds the photo) |
  | `people/{userId}/photos/{photoId}` | `SetProfile`, copying the user's upload of it (`UserDO.setProfile`) | the User DO's usual photo (`profile.photo_id`), and `going_photos` (one replaced, or one being copied in, which its alarm deletes) | `notUsual` (from before uploads were kept apart), `userGone` (no D1 `user` row) |
  | `uploads/people/{userId}/{photoId}` | the user's upload of their own photo (`PUT /v1/me/photos/{id}`) | none: the bucket's lifecycle rule deletes it a day after (What keeps the bucket clean, below) | `unclaimed` (past the grace: the rule is missing) |
  | `previews/images/{imageId}` | a link preview's picture (`link-preview.ts`) | a words line's `chat_lines.content.preview.imageId`, in any group | `unlinked` |
  | `previews/pages/{sha256}.json` | a page's preview, cached for a day | none: a cache, which the bucket's lifecycle rule deletes | `expired` (older than its day, past the grace: the rule is missing) |
  | anything else | nothing | nothing | `unknown` |

  An object uploaded within `AUDIT_GRACE_MS` (two days) is `recent`, not judged: longer than anything stays unnamed on purpose. An upload not yet sent goes after `chatRules.unsentAfterMs`, and a link preview's picture is handed out by its cached page for a day after it is stored, and a device may send it with its line after that. An older object something comes to name while the audit runs (a profile set to a photo uploaded long before) can still be reported, so the report is for reading: deleting judges each key again. A key under a known prefix that does not match its shape is `unknown`.

  It goes an owner at a time. The owners are found by listing the bucket with a `/` delimiter (`groups/`, `people/`, and the top for the rest), page by page; R2 gives 1,000 keys a page. Each group's objects are listed and judged against its Group DO, then each user's against their User DO, 20 owners at a time, and last every other prefix (`previews/` among them) against every line's preview pictures that the groups gave. Only counts, bytes and samples are kept as it goes, with one owner's keys at a time, so memory grows with the number of preview pictures, not with the bucket. A Durable Object is asked only when it must exist, so the audit makes none by asking: a group in D1's `invites` (every group has one live invitation) or `support_chats`, or one with a photo key the server wrote, which its DO noted before it stored it; and a user with a D1 `user` row. A group not asked is `groupGone`. One run takes a subrequest for each owner asked and each page listed, within the Worker's 10,000 a request until the bucket holds several thousand owners.

  **削除**: a button beside 再実行 names how many objects and bytes it deletes (the audit's `deletable`: its orphans but `unknown`), and asks in a dialog, by verdict, before it deletes, as nothing brings them back. It runs `cleanBucket` (`apps/server/src/bucket-audit.ts`) as of the time of the audit shown, which the dialog's form sends (the site refuses one that is not a time), and walks the bucket as the audit does and judges each object the same way, so it deletes what that audit reports, never a `referenced` or `recent` one, and nothing that passed the grace since the page was read. `unknown` keys stay for Pochical's people to look at first. Each owner's orphans go to their owner as the walk reaches it, which judges each again just before it deletes it and skips one something came to name since:
  - a group's photo by its Group DO (`deleteOrphanPhotos`), inside `blockConcurrencyWhile`, so no upload, send or copy reaches the group between the judging and R2's delete. A photo `sent` that no line carries is noted `takingBack` before R2 deletes it, as a line taken back leaves its photos: the note stays, so no upload stores its id again, and the sweep, set for at once, deletes it again should R2 refuse here and notes it `takenBack`. A `takenBack` note stays as it is. An `unnoted` photo gets no note. One the sweep holds (unsent, shown or deleting) is the sweep's and is never deleted here, the group set up or not.
  - a user's own photo by their User DO in the same way, but their usual photo; one of a user with no D1 `user` row by the Worker, which looks for the row again first.

  What starts naming one of these looks for it where the delete cannot come between, so nothing names a photo the delete took: the User DO copies a new usual photo from the user's upload within the same `blockConcurrencyWhile` turn that sets it (`setProfile`, INVALID_ARGUMENT when gone), and a group mark of one's own photo is set only once the group's copy is stored, the copy failing as INVALID_ARGUMENT when the upload has gone since it was looked for. A group's copy is noted `shown` before it is copied, so the group's delete leaves it.
  - a preview's picture and an expired page by the Worker, against every line's preview pictures that the groups gave on the same walk. A line can name a picture by its id whenever a device sends it, so the grace, not a check, is what keeps a picture a device still holds.

  R2 deletes 1,000 keys a call; an owner's batch R2 refuses, or an owner that cannot be asked to delete, leaves its objects as failed. An owner the walk cannot read stops the run, and the page says it could not delete; what was deleted before stays deleted. R2 deletes a key it no longer has as it does one it has, so a run cut short is run again and deletes the rest, and a run again after one that finished deletes nothing. It answers what it deleted, skipped and failed, by verdict, with counts and bytes; the page shows that and runs the audit again. One run takes the audit's subrequests and one more for each owner with orphans and each batch of the rest's; one sent without JavaScript is a page's request, which reads the audit again in it, and so takes the audit's twice.

- **What keeps the bucket clean.** The audit and its delete are for reading what is there; R2 comes to hold nothing nothing names by how each object is written and let go, not by a sweep over the bucket:
  - Every group photo is noted in `group_photos` before it is stored, and the Group DO stores it itself (`storePhoto`, `sharePhoto`), so its alarm's sweep knows of each write still on its way: having marked photos going, the sweep waits on those writes before it deletes, so none lands after the delete. A copy noted again while the sweep deletes it is `reviving`: the sweep deletes it still and copies it in anew, whenever it next runs. A note goes only once R2 has deleted its photo: a line taken back, a face or mark no longer shown, a member gone, and a send recalled before it came each mark their photos (`takingBack`, `deleting`) in the transaction that lets them go and set the alarm in the same turn, which the storage keeps together (an alarm already running then leaves it set); the sweep deletes them, then notes them `takenBack` or forgets them. A sweep that R2 refuses comes back by its alarm after `retryWait` from 10 seconds up to an hour, for as long as it takes.
  - A group erased (`eraseGroup`, as its last member's account goes) has its Group DO delete `groups/{groupId}/` before its tables, no photo noted meanwhile, so an erase cut off part way leaves the notes for the erase run again.
  - A user's upload of their own photo lands under `uploads/`, which the bucket's lifecycle rule deletes a day after it is stored: an upload never set, or a mark's, which only the group's copy keeps, goes so. `SetProfile` copies the one it sets to `people/{userId}/photos/`, noted in the User DO's `going_photos` before it is stored and let off in the transaction that names it; the photo it replaces is noted there in that transaction. The User DO's alarm deletes what `going_photos` holds, then its rows, inside `blockConcurrencyWhile`, coming back after `retryWait` should R2 refuse. Deleting the account has the User DO delete `people/{userId}/` and `uploads/people/{userId}/` before its tables, inside the `blockConcurrencyWhile` every write of one runs in, so none lands after; once erased it sets no photo.
  - A page's cached preview (`previews/pages/`) is deleted by the bucket's lifecycle rule a day after it is stored, as it is read again after that anyway.
  - A link preview's picture (`previews/images/`) is shared by every line, in any group, that a cached page handed it to, so nothing knows when the last one goes; the audit's delete is how they go for now (POC-161).

  The bucket's lifecycle rules, set on `pochical-photos` in Cloudflare (R2 › the bucket › Settings › Object lifecycle rules, or `wrangler r2 bucket lifecycle add`): delete objects under `uploads/` 1 day after they are stored, and under `previews/pages/` 1 day after.

- **全消去**: before release only, while the apps' testers have agreed to it, everything the server holds erased, so it starts as new (`apps/server/src/reset.ts`). To be removed before release (POC-155).
  - The page says what the server holds, read as it opens and again after each run: the users it would erase (those anonymous among them), the groups (each with a live invitation, as every group set up has one, or with objects under `groups/{groupId}/` in the bucket), the chats with Pochical's people, the reports, and the photos bucket's objects with their bytes. Reading it erases nothing.
  - It erases once 全消去 is typed and sent: a press alone erases nothing, the browser asks for the word before sending, and the site refuses a form without it. The page then says when it erased, over what is left, which a run that finished leaves none of; one that stopped part way says so, and the site's Worker logs why.
  - Each user is erased as deleting their account erases them (`eraseUser`, spec/sync-protocol.md, Deleting an account): their part in each group they were ever in, a group they were the last of gone whole, their chat with Pochical's people, their User DO, and last their sign-ins. Apple's tokens are not revoked: revoking takes a fresh authorization code from the person, which only the app's deletion has, so a person signing in with Apple again is a new user. A user with a chat in Slack has its thread told that their account is deleted, as a deletion tells it. The users are those with a D1 `user` row, and those with a chat with Pochical's people indexed in D1 (`support_chats`, `support_slack`) that an erasure cut off part way left.
  - Then each group still kept that no user's erasure reached, erased as a group whose last member went is (`eraseGroup`): one with a live invitation, or with objects under `groups/{groupId}/` in the bucket, which a group's erasure deletes only after its Group DO, so one cut off part way is found again. Then D1's reports and better-auth's verifications under way, and last every object left in the bucket, whatever its key (`previews/` and keys no part of the server writes among them), with no grace: nothing is to stay.
  - Each step may be run again: a run cut short is run again and erases what it left, and a run after one that finished erases nothing. A run takes about a dozen subrequests a user and one a group, and two a thousand objects, within the Worker's 10,000 a request for the few hundred users before release. A device still holding a session from before is answered 401 (spec/sync-protocol.md, Reconnecting).
  - A Durable Object nothing in D1 or the bucket names is not reached: the Worker reaches one by its name, and Cloudflare's API lists a namespace's objects only by id, to an account's token the server does not hold. The server's steps today leave none with data but what its own alarm sweeps: a User DO has its D1 `user` row until it is erased, a Group DO its invitation, and its photos, until it is erased, a support chat its `support_chats` row; a join under way as its group is erased may note the joiner's photo in the erased DO before it is refused, which its sweep deletes as any shown photo nothing names (`GroupDO.sweepPhotos`). Code from before them could: an account deleted before its support chat was erased with it (2026-10-09 to 10-17) left that chat's Group DO, with no D1 row and no way to it but the user's session, which went with them. What such a DO holds no one reads. Erasing every object of a class whatever names it is Cloudflare's `deleted_classes` migration, a deploy of its own, which this does not do.

## Slack

Pochical's people also read and answer the chats in their Slack channel (`apps/server/src/slack.ts`), through a Slack app of their own.

- Each user's chat is a thread. Their first line starts it in the channel, with the app and device and the way to the chat on the admin site; each line after it, words or a photo, stays in the thread, not sent to the channel too, so the channel holds one message a chat. Pochical's people see later lines through Slack's followed threads (a thread one has answered in is followed) and the admin list's 未返信. The thread is kept in D1's index of the chats (`support_chats.slack_thread_ts`). The chat's Group DO posts there as it tells its changes (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat); past `SUPPORT_LIMIT` (`apps/server/wrangler.jsonc`) a minute, a user's lines stay in the chat and on the admin pages but are not posted, so no one floods the channel.
- A reply in the thread by one of Pochical's people is an answer, as one from the admin site is: a line of theirs in the chat, told to the user at once, keeping the reply's message so it is not posted again, and marked ✅ in Slack; one that cannot be sent (empty, too long, or with a photo or file, which the chat does not take yet) is answered in the thread with why. One also sent to the channel is an answer all the same. Slack's links, mentions and escapes become plain words first, and its emoji, which Slack's text writes by name (`:pray:`), the characters its rich text gives; a workspace's own emoji stay as written. Slack sends an event again when unsure it arrived: the message's own id keeps it once. Replies by bots, edits, and lines outside a thread or the channel are not answers.
- An answer from the admin site is put in the thread too, so it holds the whole chat. A user's words go to Slack as written, never as a mention or a link; a reply quotes the start of what it is to, a line of photos as the apps word it (📷 写真, or 📷 写真{n}枚 for several, spec/chat.md Photos), as the admin site's lists and quotes do too. Each line's message in Slack is kept, by the chat's Group DO and in D1 (`support_slack`), for what Pochical's people do to it there.
- The user's reactions show on their lines' messages, as Slack's emoji of the same name (the app's six and others common, `apps/server/src/slack.ts`); one Slack has no name for here is said in the thread (ユーザーが 🦄 を付けました：…), and that notice deleted as it comes off; the DO tells reactions one change after another, from what it last told, so putting one on and off again leaves at most one notice. A line the user takes back reads 「（ユーザーが送信を取り消しました）」 in Slack. A user's words go whole: Slack truncates a message's text past 40,000 characters (chat.postMessage), and a line is at most `textLimits.chatMessage` characters, which the server holds to that many times 32 UTF-16 units (spec/text-limits.md); escaping turns each & < > into at most five units, and each of them starts a character of its own, so the longest post, a thread's first message with the device and the way to the admin site, is under 37,000 characters, counted as UTF-16 units or code points alike.
- An emoji Pochical's people put on a user's line in Slack, or take off, is their reaction in the chat (`reaction_added`, `reaction_removed`), the app's own bot's aside; one with no emoji known here stays in Slack. An answer deleted in Slack is taken back from the user's chat too (`message_deleted`). An answer edited in Slack is not changed in the chat.
- A user's line of photos is uploaded into their thread as files of the app's, all of them in one message (`files.getUploadURLExternal` for each, then one `files.completeUploadExternal`), and the line keeps the message they were shared in (`files.info`). A reply in the thread with images sends them to the user as one line of photos before its words (spec/chat.md, Albums), each as Slack's 1024 pixel picture of it (or the image itself when it has none and fits `chatRules.photoMaxBytes`), at most `chatRules.photosPerSend`; other files, and images past that, are answered in the thread as not sent. Each line one reply makes is notified as a chat's line is. An answer deleted in Slack takes back each line it was, its photos with it.
- A new report is posted in the channel whole, so it is read there: the reason as the app's sheet words it, whether a line or a member was reported, the group's name, who reported and who was reported by their names in the group, and for a line, it and the few around it as the report keeps them (each writer's name and words, mentions as names, a photo, shared days or a line taken back said so, the reported one marked ▶), with the way to the reports on the admin site.
- Slack reaches the server at `api.pochical.app/slack/events` (the Events API, not Socket Mode: `message.channels` or `message.groups`, `reaction_added` and `reaction_removed`), each request signed with the app's signing secret and refused otherwise. The app posts with its bot token (`chat:write`, `files:read`, `files:write`, `reactions:read`, `reactions:write`, and the history scope its message event needs, `channels:history` or `groups:history`, which also lets the relay look for a line whose answer was lost). Each line's message carries message metadata of the event type `pochical_support_line`, which Slack keeps and gives back in the history (`include_all_metadata`) with nothing registered in the app's manifest, whose schema takes no `metadata` key; should Slack warn of a post (the Worker logs `Slack warned of a relay post`), a line whose answer was lost may be posted again. A line is marked as being posted from its first try until its message is kept (`support_relay.posting_seq`): each try after the first looks for it in Slack before posting, its message by that metadata and a photo by its file's name (`photo-{seq}.jpg`), whatever Slack answered of the try before (a photo's upload refused after an earlier try may have shared it), so a retry never posts it twice. A line taken back, or past `SUPPORT_LIMIT`, before its message was kept is not posted, but looked for the same way should a try have posted it: one found is kept, and a line taken back says so there.
- The server's secrets `SLACK_BOT_TOKEN`, `SLACK_SIGNING_SECRET` and `SLACK_CHANNEL_ID` name them. Without them nothing is posted and no reply is taken; Slack failing never fails what the user did, which is answered first; a line Slack did not take is posted again later, the lines after it waiting (spec/sync-protocol.md, Chat).
- Slack not taking lines shows on the サポート list, since it may not pass by itself (the app taken out of the channel, its token revoked): a chat whose lines wait for Slack says Slack未送信, and over the list one notice says since when the earliest has waited, how many chats, and Slack's word for why (`not_in_channel`, `invalid_auth`, `ratelimited`, ...), until Slack takes them. The chats can be read and answered on the admin site meanwhile. The Worker's logs say each failed try in one line (`Support relay failed`, with the user, Slack's word, whether it passes by itself, the tries in a row, since when, and the wait before the next).
